# Touchpoints of the Commute

Every moment of the journey found in the research notes, from the first thought about travelling to the arrival, classified as direct, indirect or missed interactions. Built with the prompt in [01_Generated_Touchpoint_Prompt.md](../Prompts/01_Generated_Touchpoint_Prompt.md), on all the notes listed in [Index.md](../../Index.md). This file is the index: one row per touchpoint, linked to its own file in [./](./), which holds when exactly it happens and the notes and quotes behind it. The same moment found in several notes is one touchpoint with several citations.

- **Direct:** contact with a mobility service (app, board, train, bus, staff, ticket machine, gate, parking, taxi, sharing vehicle, ticket).
- **Indirect:** a moment around the journey that shapes it without touching a service. The field "when exactly it happens", in the touchpoint file, says which part of the journey the choice affects; where the note gives no link to a single trip, the cell says so.
- **Missed:** a moment where the person could have used a service and did not, shown explicitly in the note; the touchpoint file names what was skipped and why, where the note gives it. Silence in a note is never read as a missed interaction.
- **Quotes**, in the touchpoint files, are taken from the notes as written, shortened with "…"; translations are working translations. In 4 interview rows the wording is also quoted from the automatic transcript, which was read in full. Survey counts are of the 17 Belgian answers in the first export.
- Nothing here is a finding about how often a moment happens: a row can rest on one person.

## Contradictions

Both rows are kept; the conflict is a finding, not a failure.

1. **Whether a timely alert on one's own train exists, and whether people want one.** [Marco_Palermo_notes](../../01_Research/04_Interviews/Marco_Palermo_notes.md) says "non esistono modalità di aggiornamento su ritardi, soppressioni eccetera che sia[no] effettivamente … tempestiv[e]" ([TP-035](TP-035_using-alerts-usual-trip.md)); a friend in [Matteo_Rizzetto_notes](../../01_Research/04_Interviews/Matteo_Rizzetto_notes.md), a weekly traveller in [OR-013](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Online_Reviews/OR-013_apple-app-store_2026_sncf-connect-fr.md) and the writer in [OF-002](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Online_Forums/OF-002_eisenbahnforum_2016_eisenbahnforum-reiseerlebnisse-bahn.md) get such alerts and use them ([TP-032](TP-032_receiving-alert-usual-train.md)). In the same base 8 of 17 Belgian respondents do not want them ([survey_results](../../01_Research/05_Survey/07_survey_results.md), Q14). The operators may differ.
2. **Which channel tells the truth.** One interviewee finds a third-party app "più veritieri" than the operator and the boards, and reviewers in [OR-010](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Online_Reviews/OR-010_apple-app-store_2026_italo-it.md) and [OR-013](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Online_Reviews/OR-013_apple-app-store_2026_sncf-connect-fr.md) go to other sites ([TP-038](TP-038_checking-another-app-site.md)); among Belgian respondents the operator's app is the source believed most often, 5 of 17 ([TP-090](TP-090_choosing-source-believe-they.md)). Inside [OF-001](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Online_Forums/OF-001_ferrovie-it-forum_2019_ferrovie-it-trenitalia-sues-delay-app.md) one user calls the operator's app 'fatta coi piedi' while another judges the incumbent's train-status service 'eccellente dal punto di vista dei contenuti'.
3. **The same app, warned early and not warned at all.** In [OR-008](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Online_Reviews/OR-008_apple-app-store_2026_ns-app-nl.md) one traveller gets a delay message 'meestal nog eerder dan via de stationsmededelingen' ([TP-032](TP-032_receiving-alert-usual-train.md)), another reads in the app that trains run 'maar die rijden dus niet' ([TP-090](TP-090_choosing-source-believe-they.md)).
4. **Too many notifications and none.** In [OR-009](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Online_Reviews/OR-009_apple-app-store_2026_trenitalia-app-it.md) one commuter turns notifications off because 8-10 identical ones arrive every morning ([TP-034](TP-034_turning-off-ignoring-operator.md)), another set them for the usual train and they never arrive ([TP-033](TP-033_alerts-set-usual-train.md)).
5. **Checking before leaving.** None of the three interviewees checks on a normal day, and 38% of British rail passengers 'just turned up' ([TP-030](TP-030_checking-anything-before-going.md)); 9 of 17 Belgian respondents always check and 43% of British passengers checked live departures ([TP-029](TP-029_checking-live-departures-before.md)). In [survey_results](../../01_Research/05_Survey/07_survey_results.md), checking did not mean knowing in time: of the 9 who always check, 1 learned of the last problem before leaving home.
6. **Whether knowing before the station changes what people do.** In [AP-028](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Academic_Papers/AP-028_shires_2018_impact-planned-disruptions-rail.md) awareness before arriving at the station 'does not seem to have a large impact', and in [AP-023](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Academic_Papers/AP-023_mo_2022_inferring-passenger-responses-urban.md) 11% of passengers not yet in the system delayed their departure ([TP-093](TP-093_reacting-before-entering-station.md)); in [AP-009](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Academic_Papers/AP-009_leng_2020_role-information-availability-passengers.md) a simulation gives a median delay of 120 minutes with no information against 3 minutes with timely information. AP-009 has no row because it simulates passengers and records no lived moment; the situations also differ (planned works against an unplanned blockage).
7. **Choosing the means.** In [GR-037](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Global_Reports/GR-037_imove-australia_2021_sydney-maas-trial-final-report.md) and [AP-014](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Academic_Papers/AP-014_smith_2023_mobility-as-service-users.md) people decide the means before opening any planner ([TP-012](TP-012_deciding-means-before-opening.md)); in [AP-019](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Academic_Papers/AP-019_stromberg_2018_inviting-travelers-smorgasbord-sustainable.md) the list in the app made participants choose each day 'which mode is good for me today' ([TP-013](TP-013_choosing-each-day-means.md)).
8. **The same compensation process, easy and slow.** In [OR-007](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Online_Reviews/OR-007_apple-app-store_2026_db-navigator-de.md) compensation is 'tatsaechlich gut gemacht und leicht zu finden' while others wait weeks for it ([TP-104](TP-104_claiming-compensation-after-delay.md)).
9. **The replacement bus, told and not told.** In [Marco_Palermo_notes](../../01_Research/04_Interviews/Marco_Palermo_notes.md) "a backup was offered at once"; in [OF-002](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Online_Forums/OF-002_eisenbahnforum_2016_eisenbahnforum-reiseerlebnisse-bahn.md) 'Anscheinend weiß niemand so genau, welcher Bus nun wann wohin fährt' ([TP-098](TP-098_taking-replacement-bus.md)). They are different events.

## Coverage gaps

Named, not filled.

- **Phases.** The last mile has 7 touchpoints, the fewest of any phase. The return trip has few rows of its own: the return train decided the day before, leaving work, the last service of the night and the fallback bus on the way back; nobody describes the evening trip home leg by leg.
- **Modes.** Carpooling has no touchpoint: the only evidence is a share of commuters ([GR-024](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Global_Reports/GR-024_istat_2018_spostamenti-quotidiani-e-nuove-forme-di-mobilita.md), 7.4%) with no moment. Motorbikes have none; a survey respondent noted the option was missing. Taxi and ride-hailing rest mostly on one Korean review sample ([OR-011](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Online_Reviews/OR-011_apple-app-store_2026_kakao-t-kr.md)). E-scooters appear only as a reader's 'E-Roller kaufen' in [OF-002](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Online_Forums/OF-002_eisenbahnforum_2016_eisenbahnforum-reiseerlebnisse-bahn.md). The car as the main leg rests on one review sample, one interview, one survey answer and one statistic. Park and ride appears only as a fallback.
- **Observation.** The base has no observation notes; [GR-029](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Global_Reports/GR-029_transport-focus_2024_user-voice-disruption-management-week.md) announces an observation of disruption handling with no findings. Every touchpoint comes from what people said or wrote, or from what a survey counted.
- **One source carries much of the disruption evidence:** [OF-002](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Online_Forums/OF-002_eisenbahnforum_2016_eisenbahnforum-reiseerlebnisse-bahn.md), twelve years of one German enthusiast's travel diaries, is cited in 36 rows.
- **Contexts over-represented:** Italy in the interviews (Lombardy, Veneto and the Swiss border near Lugano; all three participants are colleagues of the interviewer and none has a fixed arrival time); Belgium in the survey (17 of 18 kept answers, French-speaking, recruited through one rail users' association); Germany through [OF-002](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Online_Forums/OF-002_eisenbahnforum_2016_eisenbahnforum-reiseerlebnisse-bahn.md); rail throughout.
- **Contexts under-represented:** France and Spain at person level only through one review sample and urban focus groups; the United States through one review sample and an old report; Japan through workshops in small towns; South Korea through taxi reviews; rural commuters; car commuters; people with fixed hours (4 survey respondents).
- **Notes with no touchpoint:** the 17 product notes (they describe what products claim, not moments of a journey), the 10 articles, most official statistics and legal texts, a laboratory experiment ([AP-022](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Academic_Papers/AP-022_ramirez_2020_travel-time-bounded-rationality.md)), a simulation ([AP-009](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Academic_Papers/AP-009_leng_2020_role-information-availability-passengers.md)), a study of leisure trips ([AP-018](../../01_Research/03_Desk_Research/Doc_Notes/Academic_Papers/AP-018_vos_2018_people-travel-their-preferred.md)), and four notes with no extracted content (AP-002, AP-020, GR-022, GR-036).
