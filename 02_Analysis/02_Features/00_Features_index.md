# Features Proposed from the Touchpoints

The minimum set of features a service could offer at the moments in [00_Touchpoints_index.md](../01_Touchpoints/00_Touchpoints_index.md): for each moment, the smallest capability that answers what the quotes show people living. Where several moments need the same capability, they share one feature. Nothing comes from competitors or from what apps usually offer. This is a proposal: what to build is Marco's decision.

- **Impact** is the number of touchpoints a feature answers: **High** for 8 or more, **Medium** for 4 to 7, **Low** for 1 to 3. The table is ordered by impact.
- **[strong] or [weak]** follows the evidence behind the touchpoints, not the appeal of the feature. A touchpoint is strong when its graded desk notes reach `medium` or `high` under section 12 of `../../01_Research/02_Plan/02_desk_research_instructions.md` (at least one grade A note, or two grade B notes). Interview and survey notes are not graded yet (`grade: n/a`), so on their own they leave a touchpoint weak. A feature takes the mark of the strongest touchpoint it answers; the touchpoints that make it strong are named in the table.
- **This file is the index.** Each feature has its own file in [./](./), which holds the evidence (note + quote) copied from the touchpoints, which were checked against the notes; "…" marks a cut. Translations are working translations.
- **What it does** is one line of what, not how.
- **[value not traced]:** no outcome the person seeks could be traced in the notes when the user stories were written; the feature has no story. F-14 and F-17 were split then. The reasons are in [00_User_Stories_index.md](../03_User_Stories/00_User_Stories_index.md).

## Contradictions that touch the features

The features are kept; the conflict is part of the evidence. Numbers refer to the contradictions in [00_Touchpoints_index.md](../01_Touchpoints/00_Touchpoints_index.md).

- **[F-03](F-03_early-warning-usual-trip.md), early warning.** Some commuters rely on alerts and one says no timely alert exists (contradiction 1); in one sample the same app sends too many and none (contradiction 4); 8 of 17 Belgian respondents do not want alerts on their usual trip. Checking does not mean knowing in time (contradiction 5).
- **[F-01](F-01_real-state-vehicle-source.md), the real state of the vehicle.** Which source is believed differs between people and samples (contradictions 2 and 3).
- **[F-04](F-04_one-search-one-booking.md) against [F-11](F-11_usual-trip-glance.md).** [F-11](F-11_usual-trip-glance.md) opens on the means already chosen, as most trial participants did (GR-037); [F-04](F-04_one-search-one-booking.md) shows every way, as the daily list did in AP-019 (contradiction 7). Both are kept.
- **[F-02](F-02_ways-still-open-trip.md) and [F-03](F-03_early-warning-usual-trip.md), knowing early.** For planned works, knowing before the station changes little in AP-028; a simulation gives a large saving (contradiction 6).

## Decisions taken

- **Minimum means shared.** When several moments need the same capability, one feature answers them all; this is what gives a feature its impact. The alternative, one feature per touchpoint, was the previous version and was set aside by the prompt's "minimum set".
- **The strength rule.** The touchpoints file gives no confidence per row, so section 12 was applied to the grades of the notes each touchpoint cites; interview and survey notes count as ungraded.
- **Impact counts touchpoints only.** It does not weigh how strong they are or how many people they concern; the strength mark and the evidence column show that.
