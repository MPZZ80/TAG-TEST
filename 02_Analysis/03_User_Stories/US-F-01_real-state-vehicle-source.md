# Story F-01

One story of [00_User_Stories_index.md](00_User_Stories_index.md), where the personas, the splits and the features sent back are explained.

As the Como commuter, standing on the platform watching the delay on the board grow from 5 to 10 to 15 minutes while a third-party app shows another time, I want to see where the train really is, when it will really leave and arrive, why it is late and which source says so, so that he knows as early as an unofficial app would tell him.
