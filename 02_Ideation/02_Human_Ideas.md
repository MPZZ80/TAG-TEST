S01 - A system that gathers all the delays inherent to the user's leg and communicates them as soon as they are available, on the user's preferred channel of communication (WhatsApp, SMS, phone call), the notifications can be filtered by type (delay, incidents, works on the line) and by day.

S02 - A system that gathers delay information by:

- comparing the position of the mobile device of the users that bought a certain train and the supposed position of the train
- comparing the timestamp of the mobile device of the users that bought a certain train and the supposed arrival time of the train in a certain location

and then communicates the delay to the user on the preferred channel.

S03 - A system that gathers delay information thanks to users that signal the delay directly on the app. The app prompts the user to give the feedback if they have not done it yet.

S04 - The user can easily send an update on their trip to relatives and friends, or set, for a given trip, that a relative or friend is alerted directly on a chosen channel, even through our WhatsApp or SMS as if the message came from the user.

S05 - Users are alerted on the state of the line in the days before the trip too, with information on strikes, maintenance works on the line or anything else that could prevent a smooth journey.

S06 - In case of delays or trip disruption, whether in the moment or in the days before the trip, the app shows the alternatives for the trip, public and private transport, and estimates the delays on each alternative from past trips and the comments of other users (something like the traffic estimates of Google Maps, but for public transport).
