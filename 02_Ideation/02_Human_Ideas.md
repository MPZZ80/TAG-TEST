S01 - A system that gathers all delays inherent to the users leg and communicates them as soon as is available to the user with their preferred channel of communication (whatsapp, sms, phonecall), the notifications can be filtered by type (delay, incidents, works on the line) and day

S02 - A system that gathers delay information by:

- comparing the position of the mobile device of the users that bought a certain train and the supposed position of the train
- comparing the timestamp of the mobile device of the users that bought a certain train and the supposed arrival time of the train in a certain location. and then communicates the delay to the user on the preferred channel

S03 - A system that gathers delay information thanks to users that signal the delay directly on the app Obviously the app stimulates the user to give the feedback... if not done

S04 - The user can easily send an update on their trip to relatives and friends, or set, for a given trip, that a relative or friend is alerted directly on a chosen channel, even through our WhatsApp or SMS as if the message came from the user

S05 - Users are alertd on the state of the line also on the days before, getting information on strikes, maintenance works on the line or other things that could prevent them to have a seren travel

S06 - in case of delays ot trip disruption wheter it is in the moment or days before the trip: the app shows any alternatives on the trip calculating delays on the alterantives based on the history and comments of the other users, on public and private transports (something like the traffic estimation of google maps but for public transports)
