# Specifications: Overview

24 user stories, all with priority concept, none outside: every story's question is answered by at least one concept of a main family in [06_Chosen_concept.md](../06_Chosen_concept.md). They are covered by 17 specifications: the foundation and 16 behaviours. No story is left out. Written with [Spec_instructions.md](Spec_instructions.md).

Read [SPEC-01_foundation.md](SPEC-01_foundation.md) first: it is the ground every other specification stands on. Then open only the specification you need.

## Splits and groupings

- **Grouped:** F-11 with F-24 (SPEC-05: the first thing shown, nothing in front); F-02 with F-19 (SPEC-06: choosing among the ways left when the trip breaks); F-06 with F-22 (SPEC-08: the connection ahead); F-04 with F-10 (SPEC-09: every way door to door); F-05, F-07 and F-13 (SPEC-10: times that can be kept); F-15, F-17a and F-20 (SPEC-14: the state of a place at that hour); F-18 with F-21 (SPEC-17: paid sessions still running). Each specification says why its stories belong together.
- **Split across specifications:**
  - F-04: showing every way (SPEC-09) and booking as one journey (SPEC-11) can ship apart.
  - F-06: whether the connection holds (SPEC-08) and warning the people met (SPEC-07).
  - F-02: the ways out (SPEC-06), whether the bus was seen (SPEC-03) and calling home (SPEC-07).
  - F-01, F-07, F-15, F-17a, F-19, F-20: their facts from travellers on the spot are collected by SPEC-03, and used in the specification that serves the rest of the story.
- **Outside stories:** none. All 24 stories are concept.
- **Background concepts used because a story asks for them:** ticket validity on a way out (C03-04) and expected fullness (C22-01) in SPEC-06; asking the connection to wait (C25-01, C25-02) in SPEC-08; tightness of a change (C10-03) and how late a run usually is (C16-04) in SPEC-10; moving a ticket with no signal (C11-01) in SPEC-11; the claim prepared from the trip (C19-04) in SPEC-13; the state of station facilities (C18-01, C20-01, C20-02, C20-11, C23-01, C23-02) in SPEC-14; tolls and stickers (C17-04, C17-06) in SPEC-16; return zones (C24-01) in SPEC-17.
- **Foundation review:** sessions (parking, shared bike) were moved out of the foundation into SPEC-17, the only specification that uses them.
