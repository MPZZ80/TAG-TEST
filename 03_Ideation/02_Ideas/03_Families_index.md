# Human and AI Ideas: Families of Concepts

The concepts of [01_Human_Ideas_index.md](01_Human_Ideas_index.md), written by Marco, and of [02_AI_Ideas_index.md](02_AI_Ideas_index.md), written by Claude, grouped by direction: two concepts are in one family when they answer the question in the same way. Nothing is added, and the families are not ranked.

- **Numbers:** [S01](S01_delays-sent-chosen-channel.md) to [S06](S06_alternatives-estimated-delays.md) are Marco's, [C01-01](C01-01_source-label.md) to [C27-12](C27-12_more-frequent-buses.md) are the AI's.
- **Families and directions are different.** The direction column of `02_AI_Ideas_index.md` was written while generating. The families were made afresh from what each concept does, so a concept can sit apart from its direction.
- **Questions:** Marco's concepts do not name a question. The section "Questions for Marco's concepts" assigns some, as Marco confirmed.
- **This file is the index.** Each family has its own file in this folder, listing its concepts; each concept has its own file with what it does.
- **[risky]** marks are those of `02_AI_Ideas_index.md`. The concepts it lists as dropped are left out.
