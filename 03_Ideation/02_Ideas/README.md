# Ideas

The concepts that answer the questions, one file each: Marco's (`S01`–`S06`), the AI's (`C01-01`–`C27-12`) and their 27 families (`FAM-01`–`FAM-27`). The marks and the chosen concept are kept whole in their own files.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `01_Human_Ideas_index.md` | The index of Marco's own concepts, S01 to S06: how delays are gathered, delivered and passed on to relatives and friends, the line state days before, and the alternatives when a trip is disrupted |
| `02_AI_Ideas_index.md` | The index of the 324 AI concepts, twelve for each of the 27 questions, in tables by supercluster and cluster, in 13 directions, with 43 marked [risky] and the concepts dropped. Not ranked |
| `03_Families_index.md` | The index of the 27 families: the 330 concepts grouped into 27 families of directions, with the author of each, the families held by one hand, the 2 unclassified and the questions assigned to Marco's concepts |
| `04_Ideas_prioritization.md` | The 330 concepts marked 0–3 on valuable, feasible and relevant, each with its reasons; the 174 concepts whose axes disagree; the 13 out; the count per axis; the same marks for the 27 families, also in order of their marks. Proposals, not a decision |
| `05_Chosen_concept.md` | The chosen concept: all 27 families, 18 main and 9 in the background (FAM-05, 10, 12, 14, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24), each with its concepts, questions, features and marks; the 27 questions with the families that answer them; where the material is thin |
