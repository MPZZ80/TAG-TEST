# S01: Delays sent on the chosen channel

One of Marco's concepts, listed in [01_Human_Ideas_index.md](01_Human_Ideas_index.md). Its marks are in [04_Ideas_prioritization.md](04_Ideas_prioritization.md).

## Marco's text

A system that gathers all the delays inherent to the user's leg and communicates them as soon as they are available, on the user's preferred channel of communication (WhatsApp, SMS, phone call). The notifications can be filtered by type (delay, incidents, works on the line) and by day.
