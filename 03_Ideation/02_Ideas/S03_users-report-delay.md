# S03: Users report the delay

One of Marco's concepts, listed in [01_Human_Ideas_index.md](01_Human_Ideas_index.md). Its marks are in [04_Ideas_prioritization.md](04_Ideas_prioritization.md).

## Marco's text

A system that gathers delay information thanks to users that signal the delay directly on the app. The app prompts the user to give the feedback if they have not done it yet.
