# S04: Trip update sent to relatives

One of Marco's concepts, listed in [01_Human_Ideas_index.md](01_Human_Ideas_index.md). Its marks are in [04_Ideas_prioritization.md](04_Ideas_prioritization.md).

## Marco's text

The user can easily send an update on their trip to relatives and friends, or set, for a given trip, that a relative or friend is alerted directly on a chosen channel, even through our WhatsApp or SMS as if the message came from the user.
