# S05: Line state days before

One of Marco's concepts, listed in [01_Human_Ideas_index.md](01_Human_Ideas_index.md). Its marks are in [04_Ideas_prioritization.md](04_Ideas_prioritization.md).

## Marco's text

Users are alerted on the state of the line in the days before the trip too, with information on strikes, maintenance works on the line or anything else that could prevent a smooth journey.
