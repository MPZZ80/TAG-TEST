# S06: Alternatives with estimated delays

One of Marco's concepts, listed in [01_Human_Ideas_index.md](01_Human_Ideas_index.md). Its marks are in [04_Ideas_prioritization.md](04_Ideas_prioritization.md).

## Marco's text

In case of delays or trip disruption, whether in the moment or in the days before the trip, the app shows the alternatives for the trip, public and private transport, and estimates the delays on each alternative from past trips and the comments of other users (something like the traffic estimates of Google Maps, but for public transport).
