## The prompt

```text
You are working in my research repository on a door to door multimodal commute.

Input: merge the files:
- Research/Generation/Human_Ideas.md - the ideas generated by me a human
- Research/Generation/AI_Ideas.md - hte ideas generated by AI
read all the ideas inside it


TASK — cluster the concepts:

1. Group the concepts by direction — families of answers, not
   topics, not features. Two concepts belong together if they
   answer the question in the same way.
2. Name each family with one line: what answer-space it covers.
3. Output a markdown table:

   ID | family | its direction, one line | concepts (by number) | authors |

   Authors: define if I wrote the idea or AI

RULES:
- Extractive: only the concepts already in the file. You invent
  nothing tonight — clustering, not creating.
- Group by direction, not by wording. "Smart alarm" and a
  "night-before planning service" may be one family; two features
  sharing the word "notification" may be two.
- Do not rank the families, do not recommend one. You name the
  space; I decide where to go.
- Keep the author marks intact. The pattern — what only I found,
  what only you found — is a finding, not noise.
- A concept that fits no family goes to "unclassified", with the
  reason. Forcing it in is lying.

FINALLY:
- Report the families that hold only one hand's concepts — mine
  alone, or yours alone. Both say something about the question.
- Count: concepts in, families out, unclassified.
```
