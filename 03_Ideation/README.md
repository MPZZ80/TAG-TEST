# Ideation

Ideas generated from the analysis: How-Might-We questions, the concepts that answer them, their families, their marks and the chosen concept.

The process was restarted on 2026-11-01. On 2026-11-03 the files were split into one file per item; the original files became the indexes at the top of each folder.

| Folder | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `00_Ideation_Prompts/` | The course's example prompts for each step of the ideation, under their own names; see its `README.md` |
| `01_How_Might_We/` | The 27 How-Might-We questions, the 9 clusters and the 3 superclusters, one file each, with their index; see its `README.md` |
| `02_Ideas/` | Marco's 6 concepts, the 324 AI concepts and the 27 families, one file each, with their indexes, the marks and the chosen concept; see its `README.md` |
| `_old/` | The first round, archived: questions, concepts, families, marks and the chosen concept. Not current; read its `README.md` before opening it |

- The specifications written from the chosen concept are in `../04_Specifications/`, outside this folder.
- Nothing here is ranked: the choice of questions and concepts is Marco's.
- Start from the index of a folder, then open only the item files you need.
- Files of this round are numbered in the order of the process: `01_`, `02_` and so on; item files are named by their ID.
