# OQ-04: Suggesting the usual trip from repeated trips

One open question of the specifications, listed in [00_Open_questions_index.md](00_Open_questions_index.md).

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Question** | May the product suggest a usual trip from trips the commuter repeats, for them to confirm, or does the commuter always enter it? |

## Why it is open

SPEC-02 lets the commuter enter the usual trip. Whether the product may also propose one is not in the stories.

## What the research says

The research shows that commuters want to save their usual trip themselves. Nothing in it says they want the product to work it out from their trips.
