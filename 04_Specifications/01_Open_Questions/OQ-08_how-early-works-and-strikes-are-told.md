## Why it is open

SPEC-03 tells works and strikes ahead of the commuter's travel days. The stories give no lead time.

## What the research says

The research shows that commuters decide on the day before or earlier (not going, or going earlier), and one review expected works to be visible four days ahead. It gives no lead time or repeat rule.
