## Why it is open

SPEC-05 opens on the trip of the day. Showing the next departures from where the commuter is would need their position.

## What the research says

The research shows that a commuter in a hurry wants the information first, and that people check departures before going to a stop. Nothing in it covers a stop away from the usual trip.
