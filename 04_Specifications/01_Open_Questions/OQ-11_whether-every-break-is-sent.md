# OQ-11: Whether every break of the trip is sent as a message

One open question of the specifications, listed in [00_Open_questions_index.md](00_Open_questions_index.md).

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Question** | When the trip of the day breaks, is a message always sent on the commuter's channel, or only for some breaks? |
| **Raised in** | [SPEC-05](../SPEC-05_ways-out-when-the-trip-breaks.md) |
| **Marker** | [not traced] |
| **Research support** | Partly supported: the research shows the situation and some signs, not the answer |
| **State** | open |
| **Decision** | none yet |

## Why it is open

SPEC-05 shows the ways out inside the product. Whether every break also triggers a message is not in the stories.

## What the research says

The research shows that late or missing warnings of a cancellation hurt, and that too many messages get turned off. It does not say which breaks deserve a message.
