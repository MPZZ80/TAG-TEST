## Why it is open

Story F-02 asks for the cost. The notes behind it record what people paid after the fact, not a wish to know the cost beforehand.

## What the research says

The research shows that ways out can cost a lot (taxis of 63 to 600 euros, new tickets), and that people add up costs when planning. No source shows someone asking to see the cost while choosing a way out. Costs were not covered in the interviews (Q-10).
