## Why it is open

SPEC-07 tells the person waiting when the arrival changes. No story gives the size of a change worth telling.

## What the research says

The research shows that telling too early or wrongly has a cost (a lift cancelled for a connection that held), and that a participant warns people once he passes his own threshold. It gives no figure.
