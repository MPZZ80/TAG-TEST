## Why it is open

In story F-09 the father drives to the station. The story asks that he knows when the commuter arrives, not when to set off.

## What the research says

The research shows a relative who drives 15 minutes to the station and has to be asked days ahead. It does not show anyone asking for the driver to be told when to set off.
