## Why it is open

SPEC-08 lists every way from door to door. The stories do not say whether a lift counts.

## What the research says

The research points to yes: a lift to the station is part of a participant's daily trip, about one trip in twenty in one paper, one Belgian respondent, and the fallback in a forum thread.
