## Why it is open

SPEC-11 books the way chosen in one step. How that works across sellers, and how the commuter pays, is a technology choice.

## What the research says

The research shows the need: people book each leg in its own app, and some avoid combining services because of separate tickets. How to book across sellers is a technology and business choice the research does not cover.
