## Why it is open

SPEC-12 compares the ticket held with the days travelled. The stories give no period.

## What the research says

The research talks about passes by the month and days by the week, and shows that people cannot predict next month. It does not fix a period. The interviews did not fully ask how many days people travel (Q-05).
