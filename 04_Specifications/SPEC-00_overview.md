# Specifications: Overview

24 user stories, all with the same weight: the chosen concept is not followed for now. They are covered by 17 specifications: the foundation, which also designs the architecture, the information architecture (17 pages, proposed), the start page and the initial set-up, and 16 behaviours. No story is left out. Written with [Spec_instructions.md](Spec_instructions.md), for a frontend-only prototype made of web components, with all data from mock datasets in `data/`.

Read [SPEC-01_foundation.md](SPEC-01_foundation.md) first: its Architecture names the shared components and datasets, its Start page and Initial set-up are where the commuter begins and enters what every other behaviour relies on. Then open only the specification you need.

## Splits and groupings

- **Grouped:** F-11 with F-24 (SPEC-04: the first view); F-02 with F-19 (SPEC-05: choosing when the trip breaks); F-06 with F-22 (SPEC-07: the connection ahead); F-04 with F-10 (SPEC-08: every way door to door); F-05, F-07 and F-13 (SPEC-09: times that can be kept); F-15, F-17a and F-20 (SPEC-13: the station at that hour); F-18 with F-21 (SPEC-16: paid sessions). Each specification says why.
- **Split across specifications:**
  - F-07: entering the own rules (SPEC-01) and planning with them (SPEC-09).
  - F-11 and F-03: entering the usual trip and days (SPEC-01) and using them (SPEC-04, SPEC-03).
  - F-04: showing every way (SPEC-08) and booking as one journey (SPEC-10).
  - F-06: whether the connection holds (SPEC-07) and warning the people met (SPEC-06).
  - F-02: the ways out (SPEC-05) and knowing when to call home (SPEC-06).
- **Written for the dependencies:** the initial set-up, in SPEC-01, answers no story on its own; it exists because SPEC-02 to SPEC-16 need the doors, usual trip, days, own rules, needs, channels and consent it provides. The trip of the day is held in SPEC-04 for the same reason.
- **Travellers reporting** is a concept (S03, FAM-03), not something a story asks for: "other travellers" is one of the sources in the foundation, and which sources are used is an open question.
