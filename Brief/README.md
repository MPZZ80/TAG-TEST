# Brief

The starting point of the project: who the user is, what the problem is and why Marco cares about it.

| File | What it is | Read it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brief.md` | The project brief, in Marco's own words | Always, before any other work. It is short |

- The focus is the intercity commute **door to door**: the whole journey, not only the leg between the two cities.
- The brief is Marco's text. Do not rewrite or extend it; add to it only what he asks, in his words.
- The brief assumes that fragmented information is the core problem. The research is asked to look for evidence against that assumption as well.
