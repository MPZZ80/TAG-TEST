## What it says

Overview of Dutch mobility in 2023 and 2024, with attention to train, bus, tram and metro, aviation and the extent of working from home.

## Data and quotes used

- The total distance travelled by residents aged 6 and over on Dutch territory was 4% lower in 2023 than in 2014, on average 11,300 km per person per year, 31 km per person per day (PDF p.2: "4% lager dan in 2014 ... gemiddeld 11.300 km per persoon per jaar"; working translation).
- In 2024, 42% of Dutch workers worked from home at least one hour a week (41% in 2023), still clearly above the roughly 30% of the years before the pandemic (PDF p.2; working translation from Dutch).
- Asked how they experience accessibility, about 95% of people say they can usually or always reach their workplace, place of study and hospital when they want to; about 5% cannot (PDF p.4).
- Average delay per train journey stayed at 2.2 minutes in 2024; train kilometres run rose 5% on 2023 (PDF p.4).
- Distance travelled by train in 2024 rose on 2023 but was still below 2014; bus, tram and metro were 4% above 2014 (PDF p.2).
- Road traffic on the whole network in 2024 was for the first time slightly above 2019 again (137.5 against 135.9 billion vehicle km); time lost on main roads rose 5.1% on 2023 (PDF p.4).
- The national travel survey changed method in 2024, so there are no reliable 2024 figures on personal travel by purpose; rail passenger figures also have breaks in 2020, 2022 and 2024 (PDF p.2-3).

## Limits

Only the summary was read; the full report was not downloaded. No 2024 data on commuting by purpose because of the break in the survey. The Netherlands only. Tables did not convert to text.
