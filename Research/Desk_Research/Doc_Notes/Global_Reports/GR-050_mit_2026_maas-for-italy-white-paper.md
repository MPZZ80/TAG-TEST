## What it says

Announces the white paper "Mobility as a Service for Italy", which describes the first large-scale MaaS experiment in Italy, promoted by the Department for Digital Transformation with the Ministry.

## Data and quotes used

- "Da oggi è disponibile 'Mobility as a Service for Italy – Il primo programma nazionale di mobilità come servizio', il white paper che racconta l'esperienza di MaaS for Italy, la prima sperimentazione su larga scala in Italia." In English: from today the white paper is available that tells the experience of MaaS for Italy, the first large-scale experiment in Italy (working translation).
- The programme cost 56.9 million euros of public money, involved 6 municipalities, 6 regions and 1 autonomous province, and 14 applications of MaaS operators (web page, 18 June 2026).
- By 31 May 2026 the users of the trial had made almost 950,000 trips, an average of over 16 trips per user, which implies about 59,000 users in total; the number of users is my calculation, not a figure in the source (web page).
- The centre of the programme is a national repository (DSRM) of static and real-time data of transport services in the European standards NeTEx and SIRI, which lets operators build planning, booking and payment of multimodal trips (web page).

## Limits

A ministry press release about its own programme: it reports only successes and no data on who used the apps, for what trips, whether they kept using them or whether commuters were among them. The white paper is linked but was not downloaded or read. The trials are in cities and regions, not designed for intercity commuting.
