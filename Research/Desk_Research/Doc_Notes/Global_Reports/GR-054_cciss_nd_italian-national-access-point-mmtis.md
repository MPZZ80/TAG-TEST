## What it says

Explains that the national access point gathers data on different modes of transport through regional access points, in the NeTEx standard with an Italian profile, following the EU delegated regulation 2017/1926.

## Data and quotes used

- "The National Access Point for Multimodal Mobility gathers information and data on different modes of transport, with the aim of facilitating the consultation of data and enabling their dissemination through a single portal, according to the European Delegated Regulation 1926/2017."
- Static data is provided by transport authorities, operators and infrastructure managers through Regional Access Points using the European standard NeTEx CEN/TS 16614 and its Italian profile (page text).
- The national access point collects timetables, network and routes, cycling and pedestrian network, park and ride, bike sharing stations and fare products, supplied through regional access points in the NeTEx standard under European Delegated Regulation 1926/2017 (web page).
- Data suppliers listed: 16 regions, the two autonomous provinces, Trenitalia, Italo and FlixBus; Basilicata, Sicily, Umbria and Valle d'Aosta are not in the list (web page, read on 2026-10-07).

## Limits

A descriptive page with no date and no figures on how complete, current or used the data are. The list of suppliers says who sends data, not that the data cover all services or include real-time information. The page speaks of static data; real-time data are mentioned only as a format received.
