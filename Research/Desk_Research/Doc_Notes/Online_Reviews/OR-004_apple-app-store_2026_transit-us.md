## What it says

A sample of 100 US App Store reviews of Transit, written in a single week and mostly negative. The dominant complaint is that predicted bus times are wrong: the bus is '5 minutes away for 15 minutes', or shown and never comes. Many blame the app for the bus service itself. A second complaint is having to pay to see more, from people who take the bus because they have little money. The app asks for a rating very often, which explains the volume.

## Data and quotes used

- Store rating on 2026-10-07: 4.62 average from 1,023,558 ratings in the United States store; app version 6.3.5, released 2026-10-06 (Apple lookup).
- Sample: 100 reviews, 5 stars: 37, 4 stars: 2, 3 stars: 8, 2 stars: 11, 1 stars: 42.
- Sample: 100 reviews, App Store United States, 2026-09-28 to 2026-10-04; ratings: 37 five-star, 2 four-star, 8 three-star, 11 two-star, 42 one-star (store sample). My count by theme, approximate: about 40 on wrong times or buses that do not come; about 8 on the paid tier; about 6 on being asked to rate; about 8 blame drivers or the bus service; about 25 short praise.
- The typical failure: 'the app that will tell you a bus is 5mins away for 15mins and make you late for work' (2026-10-03); 'it said it was going to be here in 1 min or 2, but it end up being like 10 min late' (2026-10-03); 'it told me a bus was coming and then there is no bus and says it'll be here in an hour' (2026-10-01).
- Buses that exist only on screen: 'Phantom trains ... that are on this app's schedule, but don't exist in reality' (2026-09-29); ES 'a veces detecta los buses fantasmas, es decir que se ven en la pantalla y nunca llega' (EN: sometimes it shows ghost buses, seen on the screen and never arriving) (2026-09-29); the bus 'disappears from the map' (2026-09-29).
- No word on what is blocking the bus: 'there was an accident and it blocked the route the bus was on and it never told me the bus wasn't able to come. I was standing outside for an hour and missed my plans' (2026-10-01); 'No updates on train services, being delayed or going local' (2026-10-01).
- Live position depends on other riders: it 'only shows where the bus actually is ... when someone is using the app on the specific bus' (2026-09-28); where it works: 'it does provide accurate real-time info using people riding the bus. I prefer it to apple or google maps by a mile' (2026-09-28). One city's operator does not give real-time data to the app (2026-10-04).
- Changes planned too cautiously: 'When using transfers and your arrival and departure times are close, it plans the next departure instead, thinking you can't make the transfer ... The transit service has designed arrivals and departures to align. I wish the app would let me pick a departure time earlier than the one it thinks I can make' (2026-09-29).
- Paying: 'I am taking the bus, you think I have money for a stupid subscription?' (2026-10-04); 'the whole point is for it to be cheap and convenient not have to pay money for a premium version just to plan out my day' (2026-10-02); the app gives the paid tier free on request, which some thank (2026-10-03, 09-29).
- The app is blamed for the service: 'The app would be great if bus drivers actually followed the scheduled times ... There are simply just too many issues with how the buses are actually run for this app to genuinely be helpful' (2026-09-29).
- What works for satisfied users: 'I can strategically plan out my day and make it to work on time'; a countdown on the lock screen while waiting; 'I hardly need to use my car anymore'; 'good cycling plus transit routes dynamically. Something you just can't do with' the general map apps (reviews of 2026-10-04, 10-01).

## Limits

One week of reviews, prompted by the app asking for a rating after use: a very skewed moment, probably after an update. United States, city buses, people without a car: different users from Italian commuters between cities. The app is rated for the failures of the bus service. Counts by theme are my rough reading of 100 items, not market data.
