## What it says

A sample of 100 Swiss App Store reviews of the national railway's app, mostly negative after a redesign that opens a saved trip on a map view. Users, many of them daily ones, say the map hides what they need when changing: at what time and on which platform the next train leaves. They ask for the list view back as default. Other points: connections proposed with changes too tight to make, the commuter section showing 'all green' while the commuter is stuck in a stopped train, and tickets that do not take the user's regional pass into account.

## Data and quotes used

- Store rating on 2026-10-07: 3.04 average from 979 ratings in the Switzerland store; app version 12.58.4, released 2026-10-06 (Apple lookup).
- Sample: 100 reviews, 5 stars: 8, 4 stars: 9, 3 stars: 11, 2 stars: 21, 1 stars: 51.
- Sample: 100 reviews, App Store Switzerland, 2026-06-21 to 2026-10-04; ratings: 51 one-star, 21 two-star, 11 three-star, 9 four-star, 8 five-star (store sample). My count by theme, approximate: about 20 on the new map view of the trip; about 14 on payment or purchase failing; about 10 on wrong, missing or impractical connections; about 9 on the check-in function now requiring Bluetooth and motion data; about 7 on passes not counted in the price; about 4 on advertising; about 10 praise.
- What a traveller needs at a change, DE: 'Wenn ich am Bahnhof umsteige, moechte ich auf einen Blick sehen, wann ich auf welchem Gleis sein muss und nicht eine riesen Karte mit der Ankunftszeit und Gleisangabe des Zuges (aus dem ich bereits ausgestiegen bin)' (EN: when I change at a station I want to see at a glance when I must be on which platform, not a huge map with the arrival time and platform of the train I have already left) (2026-09-08); 'Als Reisender moechte ich wissen, auf welchem Gleis ich ankomme und auf welchem Gleis der anbindende Zug faehrt' (2026-07-19).
- Regular users are not tourists: EN-original 'The default view doesn't help me with connections, too much on the screen. I want the old view as a default. I'm not a tourist' (2026-07-14); 'Das Feature der Karte brauche ich etwa 1 von 50 Fahrten' (EN: I need the map feature about once in 50 trips) (2026-10-02); 'fuer den Alltagsgebrauch nicht brauchbar, man sieht die wichtigsten Infos nicht auf einen Blick' (EN: not usable for everyday use, the most important information is not visible at a glance) (2026-08-20). The company made the list the default again in September (2026-09-12).
- Changes planned too tight, a regular traveller near Solothurn: the app always proposes the bus that leaves the least waiting at the station, 4-5 minutes before the train: 'es ist stressig umzusteigen ... Ausserdem sind die Busse am Morgen immer verspaetet und ich habe schon einige Male deshalb den Zug verpasst. Es waere besser wenn mir auch einer der frueheren Busse angezeigt werden koennte, ich muss jetzt immer separat einen Bus suchen und dann den Zug' (EN: changing is stressful ... and the buses are always late in the morning and I have missed the train several times because of it. It would be better if one of the earlier buses were also shown; now I always have to search for a bus and then the train separately) (2026-08-18). A wish to set one's own changing time in the app, possible only on the web (2026-07-19).
- First mile bus and missed train, IT: 'guardo e magari trovo un tre minuti di ritardo, scendo giu vedo il bus letteralmente un minuto dopo passarmi davanti ... c'e scritto che arriva e 30 in stazione in realta arriva ai 35, io dopo rischio sempre di perdere il treno perche non azzeccate mai le coincidenze' (EN: I look and see a three-minute delay, I go down and the bus passes in front of me one minute later ... it says it reaches the station at :30 and in fact arrives at :35, so I always risk missing the train because the connections are never right) (2026-08-26).
- The commuter section in a disruption, DE: 'Ich sitze jetzt wieder mal in der Zuercher S-Bahn fest. Bei meiner Pendelverbindung, die betroffen ist, heisst es alles gruen - fuer morgen. Das mit der Unterstuetzung fuer Pendler ueberlegt ihr euch besser nochmal' (EN: I am stuck once again in the Zurich suburban train. For my commute connection, which is affected, it says all green, for tomorrow. You had better think again about the support for commuters) (2026-06-23). The saved commute routes take minutes to load (2026-06-29); push messages on disruptions 'sind Gluecksache' (EN: are a matter of luck) (2026-08-25).
- Routes people do not accept: 'Die SBB App zeigt die besten und schnellsten Verbindungen oft nicht an. Man wird auch in Stosszeiten mit dem Bus durch die Quartiere geschickt mit der Konsequenz, dass man seinen Anschluss verpasst' (EN: the app often does not show the best and fastest connections; at peak times one is sent by bus through the neighbourhoods and misses the connection) (2026-08-22); connections missing, so users check a regional operator's app or a general map (2026-09-12, 08-07); a city bus not included, so the plan has a 16-minute walk in place of 4 minutes by bus (2026-06-30).
- The walk to the stop miscalculated: the app planned a later bus for the walk from the user's position; the user caught the earlier bus and was fined on the train because the ticket was 'not yet valid' (2026-08-05).
- Passes not counted: FR 'l'App ne tient pas toujours compte des abonnements regionaux: on se retrouve a payer des bouts de trajets inclus dans notre abonnement' (EN: the app does not always take regional passes into account: we end up paying for parts of the trip included in our pass) (2026-08-27); a competitor's pay-as-you-go app is said to charge less for this reason (2026-09-27, 09-23).
- Check-in travel made conditional on Bluetooth, motion data and location 'always': several stop using it for privacy and battery reasons (2026-08-29, 08-19, 06-26, 06-22).
- Payment failing at the last minute: 'Hatte fast den letzten Zug verpasst weil die ... App nicht funktionierte ... Ohne diesen Automaten waere ich 6 Stunden spaeter erst zuhause' (EN: I almost missed the last train because the app did not work ... without that ticket machine I would have been home 6 hours later) (2026-09-29).
- Advertising in a paid public service is rejected: 'On paie cher, mais cela fonctionne. Les publicites rompent ... ce dernier [contrat]' (EN: we pay a lot, but it works; the adverts break that unwritten contract) (2026-08-25).
- A newcomer from the car finds it helpful: 'Ayant jusqu'a present toujours voyage en voiture, je trouverais l'application d'une grande aide, simple et efficace' (2026-09-21). A traveller back from abroad calls it the best railway app (2026-07-04).

## Limits

App store reviews after a redesign, so anger about one change dominates; the company reversed it during the sample. Switzerland: the best case for integrated public transport, yet the same complaints about tight changes and late feeder buses appear, which is informative. Reviewers include daily commuters, but distances are not stated. Counts by theme are my rough reading of 100 items, not market data. Translations are working translations; some quotes are shortened.
