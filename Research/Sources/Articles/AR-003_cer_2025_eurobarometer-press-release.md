## What it says

Presents the 2024 Eurobarometer as showing a positive booking experience, and argues that the situation is improving.

## Data and quotes used

- 73% of respondents who had tried to book two or more connecting trains with different operators found the process easy; of those who do not book combined connecting trains, only 9% said the main reason was not being able to buy all tickets in one place (page text).
- 61% of travellers found it easy to book multimodal journeys combining collective modes; of those who do not book multimodal journeys, only 5% gave not being able to buy all tickets in one place as the main reason (page text).
