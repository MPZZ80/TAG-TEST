## What it says

Reports the white paper on MaaS for Italy: the government programme funded with 56.9 million euros involved 6 municipalities, 6 regions and 1 autonomous province.

## Data and quotes used

- "Il programma del governo finanziato con 56,9 milioni di euro ha coinvolto 6 Comuni, 6 Regioni e 1 Provincia autonoma, promuovendo un modello basato su interoperabilità, condivisione dei dati e adozione degli standard europei." In English: the government programme funded with 56.9 million euros involved 6 municipalities, 6 regions and 1 autonomous province, promoting a model based on interoperability, data sharing and the adoption of European standards (working translation).
- The article repeats the ministry's figures: 56.9 million euros, almost 950,000 trips by 31 May 2026, over 16 trips per user, 14 applications (web page, 18 June 2026).
- The two Living Labs in Milan and Turin involved over 1,500 citizens and attracted 1.5 million euros of further investment; they concern connected and autonomous vehicles, not commuters' journeys (web page).

## Limits

A trade-press article that rewrites the government press release (GR-050) and adds a statement by the undersecretary: it is not an independent source and must not be counted as a second confirmation. No critical view, no user data.
