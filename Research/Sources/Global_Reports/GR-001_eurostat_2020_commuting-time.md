## What it says

Where employed people carry out their main job and how long they take to get from home to work, before the COVID-19 pandemic.

## Data and quotes used

- 61.3% of workers in the EU-27 travelled from home to work in less than 30 minutes in 2019 (Highlights).
- 79% of employed people in the EU-27 mainly worked at their employer's or their own premises in 2019 (Highlights).

## Limits

Pre-pandemic data from 2019 that will not be updated. It measures home-to-work time overall, not split by leg, and does not separate intercity from local commutes.
