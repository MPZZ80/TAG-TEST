## What it says

How many employed people commute from one region to another within their own country.

## Data and quotes used

- More than 12.5 million people (6.4% of all employed) commuted to work from one region to another within their country of residence in 2022, a 4.4% increase from 2021 (article text).
- From the page-fetch summary of 2026-10-07, to be checked on the live page before any use: 12.5 million commuters between regions are 6.4% of 197 million employed people in the EU in 2022 (12.0 million in 2021). Highest shares by region of residence: Brabant Wallon 45%, Vlaams-Brabant 42%, Pest (Hungary) 41%, Namur 38%, Burgenland 36%, Niederoesterreich 29%. Highest numbers: Brandenburg 297,000; Pest 272,000; Arnsberg 248,000; Niederoesterreich 236,000; Lueneburg 223,000. All are regions around a capital or a large city. No Italian region is named in the summary (web page).
- Same summary: 2.09 million employed people (1.1%) commuted to a different country (web page).

## Limits

Regions are an administrative unit, not a proxy for trips between cities. The article gives no frequency, mode or journey time. The figures above come from an automatic summary of the page, not from the page text itself: check them on the live page before citing.
