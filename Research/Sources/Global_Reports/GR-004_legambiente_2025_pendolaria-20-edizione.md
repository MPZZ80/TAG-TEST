## What it says

Situation of public transport in Italy in 2025: infrastructure, funding, quality of service for commuters, age of regional fleets, the worst lines, transport poverty.

## Data and quotes used

- Funding for road and rail transport was about 6.2 billion euros in 2009, under 4.9 billion in 2020 and 5.345 billion in 2025 (PDF p.29, sentence cut at 5,345).
