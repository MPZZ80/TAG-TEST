## What it says

Results of the 2024 Eurobarometer on how EU citizens book and ticket multi-operator and multimodal journeys.

## Data and quotes used

- One third of respondents had never booked a multimodal or multi-operator journey; of those who had, over a third had encountered booking difficulties (COM(2026) 231, PDF p.2; see GR-007).
- Among respondents who had tried to book two or more connecting trains with different operators, 73% found it easy; 61% of travellers found it easy to book multimodal journeys combining collective modes (CER press release, AR-003).

## Limits

The press corner page opens but its text could not be read by my tool, and the original survey report was not located. The figures above come from two secondary copies that frame the same survey differently: the Commission stresses difficulties, the rail industry body stresses that most found booking easy.
