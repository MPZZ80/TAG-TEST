## What it says

Defines the problems behind the 2026 Passenger Package. It identifies four problem drivers and two market failures in online ticket distribution (PDF p.11).

## Data and quotes used

- Problem 1: lack of transparency, unfair conditions and uneven playing field in the online ticketing market. Problem 2: incomplete offer of rail tickets on rail platforms (PDF p.11).

## Limits

Scope is booking and ticketing, with a strong rail focus. Only the table of contents and the start of the problem definition were read; the evidence base in the remaining pages is still to be read.
