## What it says

Proposes rules on multimodal booking and repeals Regulation (EC) No 80/2009. The memorandum states that passengers still face significant barriers to search, compare, combine and book travel options.

## Data and quotes used

- An analysis of 100 representative routes in the EU found multimodal options available in 76% of cases, yet very few multimodal digital mobility services showed options in different modes and these were rarely combined (PDF p.2).
- The 2024 Eurobarometer of 26,000 EU citizens found that one third had never booked a multimodal or multi-operator journey, and that over a third of those who had encountered booking difficulties (PDF p.2).
