## What it says

Examines the impact of MaaS on how passenger transport is organised, and the regulatory and governance requirements for integrating MaaS with public transport.

## Data and quotes used

- MaaS is defined as a model for supplying a wide range of passenger transport services through a single digital customer interface, integrating transport, information and payment services (PDF p.6).

## Limits

Urban focus and written from the point of view of public transport authorities, from before the 2026 EU proposals. Only the first pages were read.
