## What it says

Recommendations on policy, infrastructure and integration for complementing public transport with shared mobility, with attention to transport poverty.

## Data and quotes used

- The document states that transport poverty is a result of transport injustice and that in suburban, peri-urban and rural areas those who can barely afford a car spend a disproportionate amount on it (PDF p.7, section The Challenge).

## Limits

Expert opinion, not original research. Focus on sustainability and equity more than on commuters' experience. Only the contents and first pages were read.
