## What it says

Concept of hybrid work and how it is practised across Europe, including its effects on offices and commuting.

## Data and quotes used

- Companies show a tendency to occupy office buildings in more central areas, close to employee housing, because this saves commuting time (PDF p.25).

## Limits

Qualitative review of hybrid work, with little data on intercity commuting. In my earlier candidate list I gave 2022 as its year; that was wrong. Only a few passages were read.
