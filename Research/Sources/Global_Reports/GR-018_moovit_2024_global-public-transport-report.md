## What it says

Commute times, wait times, transfers, payment and improvement factors for public transport in 50 cities.

## Data and quotes used

- What would make transit riders in the United States ride more often: higher frequency of vehicles (24%), accurate and reliable arrival times (24%), lower fares (21%) (web page).
- Average one-way commute by public transport: 52 minutes in Miami, 51 in New York, 50 in London, 47 in Paris and Los Angeles, 43 in San Francisco and Philadelphia; shortest among the 50 cities 42 minutes in Venice, longest 67 in Mexico City (web page).
- Average wait at stops per trip: 21 minutes in Miami, 14 in New York, Boston, Chicago and Philadelphia, 12 in Seattle, 10 in Paris (web page).
- Transfers: in Boston 25% of riders use three or more lines per journey; almost a third in Paris; in San Francisco 40% reach their destination on one line (web page).
- Only 15% of US transit riders prefer to pay with a digital wallet or a transit app; a transit card comes first and cash second in every US city (web page).

## Limits

A press release by an app company about its own users: data from trip requests in the Moovit app and a survey of its users, with no sample sizes or method on this page. App users planning a trip are not all commuters, and 'commute time' here is the length of trips planned in the app. Cities and their surroundings only; nothing on trips between cities. The full report was not captured, so no Italian city figures were read.
