## What it says

Daily travel for work and study in Italy in 2019 and the use of public transport, just before the pandemic.

## Data and quotes used

- In 2019, 22 million people travelled every day to go to work and 11 million to go to school; 12 million employed people and 3.5 million students moved outside their own municipality (PDF p.1).
- In 2019, 900,000 people travelled by train every day and 1 million several times a week; 3 million used tram, bus or trolleybus every day (PDF p.1).
- To go to work by public transport one person in two needed more than 30 minutes; 16.5 million employed people used only a car or motorbike, and just over 2 million, one in ten, habitually used public transport (PDF p.1, p.4).
- Half of commuters, about 18 million, moved only inside their municipality; in Lombardy more than two employed people in three work in a municipality other than their own, about 2.8 million (PDF p.3).
- Over 60% of employed people leave home between 7:00 and 8:00, and one in five before 6:30 (PDF p.4).
- Train use is mostly occasional: more than 17 million people used a train in 2019, over 13 million of them only a few times in the year (PDF p.2).
- Method: the survey Aspetti della vita quotidiana, about 25,000 households in about 800 municipalities, web and face-to-face interviews (PDF p.6).

## Limits

Pre-pandemic data. A municipality is not a proxy for intercity trips. Journey times are for the whole trip and are not split by leg.
