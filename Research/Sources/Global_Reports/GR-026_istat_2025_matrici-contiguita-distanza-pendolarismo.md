## What it says

Distributes the contiguity and distance matrices between municipalities and the commuting matrix for study or work.

## Data and quotes used

- ISTAT publishes three kinds of matrices: contiguity between municipalities and between provinces (2011-2025, published 8 October 2025); origin-destination distances in km and travel times in minutes between all 7,903 Italian municipalities (geography at 1 January 2021, road graph of 2020, released 24 May 2023); and the commuting matrices from the censuses (web page, captured 2026-10-06).
- A commuting matrix for work for the year 2021 was published on 2 October 2025. The page lists it under 'Anno 2021' as 'Matrice di pendolarismo per lavoro' (work only; the 2011 matrix covers work and study). Matrices for 2011 (also by census section, zip 126 MB), 2001 and 1991 are also offered (web page).
- Distances and times are computed on the road network for the fastest route, taking traffic and road type into account; the route there and back can differ by several km while times stay similar (web page). The matrix is a car-road measure: it gives no public transport time.

## Limits

This is a description of datasets, not a finding. No file was downloaded or analysed, so the number of people commuting between specific cities (for example over 50 km) is still not known from this source. The distance matrix is road-based only. The 2021 matrix covers work trips only according to the page title; this was not checked in the file.
