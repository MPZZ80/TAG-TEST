## What it says

The regulator's account of its work on ticket retailing, passenger information, accessible travel, complaints and compensation. The passenger information section says passengers should have accurate and timely information about their travel options, including during disruption.

## Data and quotes used

- The ORR continued to observe a mix of good and poorer practice in passenger information when rail replacement services are used; it wrote to operators again in July 2025 (page "Passenger information").

## Limits
