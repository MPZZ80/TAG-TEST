## What it says

Personal travel in 2024: trips, distance, modes and purposes, and how travel compares with before the pandemic.

## Data and quotes used

- Commuting was the second most common trip purpose in 2024, with 111 trips per person, and accounted for the longest distance travelled on average, 1,007 miles per person (main findings).
- Personal travel in 2024 remained similar to 2023 and had not returned to pre-pandemic trends (main findings).
- 58% of public transport trips in England in 2024 used more than one mode of transport and 42% a single mode; about 25% of the multi-modal public transport trips involved more than one public transport mode, 32% in London (web page, main findings).
- 64% of public transport trips with more than one mode started with walking (web page).
- Commuting was the second most common trip purpose, 111 trips per person a year, and the purpose with the longest distance, 1,007 miles per person a year out of 6,082 (web page).
- Car trips, as driver or passenger, were 59% of trips and 76% of distance; 83% of people used a private car at least once a week (web page).
- Travel had not returned to pre-pandemic trends: 922 trips per person in 2024 against 953 in 2019, and distance 6% lower; surface rail trip rates were back to a level similar to 2019 (web page).
- Method: household survey of residents of England with interviews and a seven-day travel diary; 16,980 people took part fully in 2024, response rate 32% (web page).

## Limits

England only. The page gives main findings for all trips; figures for commuting by mode, distance band or number of legs are in data tables that were not downloaded. 'More than one mode' counts walking stages, so 58% is not a measure of trips combining car, train and bus. Response rate of 32%.
