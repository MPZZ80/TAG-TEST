## What it says

Commuting behaviour in the United States in 2022, compared with 2019 and 2021.

## Data and quotes used

- 75.9% of workers drove alone to work in 2019, about 67.8% in 2021 and 68.7% in 2022 (PDF p.2).
- Average one-way travel time to work was 27.6 minutes in 2019, a 10-year low of 25.6 minutes in 2021, and 26.4 minutes in 2022 (PDF p.6).

## Limits

Pre- and post-pandemic comparison for all commuters; no split between intercity and local trips.
