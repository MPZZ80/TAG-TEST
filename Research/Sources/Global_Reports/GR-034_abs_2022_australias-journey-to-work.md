## What it says

The methods of travel Australians used to get to work on Census day, 10 August 2021, during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## Data and quotes used

- Respondents could record up to three methods of travel to work, so combined modes are visible (article text).

## Limits

Census day was during the pandemic with stay-at-home restrictions in parts of the country, so it is not a normal day. Only the introduction was read.
