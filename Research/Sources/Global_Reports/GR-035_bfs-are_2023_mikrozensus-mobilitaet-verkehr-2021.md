## What it says

Mobility behaviour of the Swiss population in 2021: vehicles and passes owned, daily distances and times, modes, purposes, day trips and attitudes to transport policy.

## Data and quotes used

- In 2021 leisure accounted for 43% of distance, followed by commuting with 28% (PDF p.19: "Mobilitätszweck waren 2021 mit einem Distanzanteil von 43% die Freizeitaktivitäten, gefolgt von den Arbeitswegen mit 28%"; working translation).
- Annual mobility including abroad was about 14,900 km per person, roughly 40% less than in 2015 (PDF p.19; working translation).

## Limits

Written in German; the quotes are in the original with my working translation, to be checked by a German reader before relying on them. 2021 was still affected by the pandemic. Only the front matter and one summary page were read.
