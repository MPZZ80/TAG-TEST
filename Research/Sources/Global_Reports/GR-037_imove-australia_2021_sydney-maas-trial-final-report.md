## What it says

Reports the first MaaS trial in Australia. Its objectives include testing service mixes and subscription plans, assessing the readiness of Sydney's transport mix, understanding uptake and willingness to pay, and testing the ability to influence travel behaviour. It says a digital platform alone is not enough to go beyond an improved journey planner.

## Data and quotes used

- The report describes itself as the first trial worldwide with transparent reported quantitative evidence on MaaS (executive summary, PDF p.6; the sentence is cut in my extraction).

## Limits

A short trial of early adopters that was cut by COVID-19. A web summary says participants valued support and feedback more than the planner and wallet, and saw little value without a financial incentive; that is not verified until the chapters on results are read. 236 pages, only the executive summary was read.
