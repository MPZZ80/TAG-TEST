## What it says

A full picture of how the French travel, locally (within 80 km of home) and over long distances (more than 80 km), which is close to the intercity commute.

## Data and quotes used

- "En 2019, les Français réalisent trois déplacements (dans un rayon de 80 km autour de leur domicile) par jour en semaine. Ils consacrent 1 h 02 à se déplacer, soit 6 minutes de plus qu'en 2008." In English: in 2019 the French make three trips a day on weekdays within 80 km of home and spend 1 h 02 travelling, 6 minutes more than in 2008 (web page summary; working translation).
- The car remains the first mode of transport and accounts for 63% of trips; outside urban units it is used for four trips out of five (web page summary; working translation).
- The French survey draws its line at 80 km: 'local mobility' is every trip within 80 km of home as the crow flies, 'long-distance mobility' every trip beyond. Regular long commutes to a usual workplace are counted inside the professional long-distance trips together with business travel (PDF p.8-9).
- A trip is described by one purpose and one main means, even when several means are used; only the main one is analysed (PDF p.8).
- On weekdays in 2019 the French spent 1 hour 2 minutes a day travelling, 6 minutes more than in 2008; people aged 25 to 54 spent almost 1 hour 15, and those aged 35 to 54 gained more than 10 minutes in ten years (PDF p.12, p.14).
- A trip by public transport lasts 41 minutes on average, against 19 minutes by car, 18 by bike and 14 on foot; all grew since 2008 (PDF p.12).
- Car is the main means for 62.8% of local weekday trips, walking 23.7%, public transport 9.1%, bike 2.7%. Outside urban areas the car makes four trips in five; in the Paris area walking (38%) and public transport (25%) both exceed or approach the car (33%) (PDF p.12).
- Trips beyond 80 km: 374.1 million in 2019, 6.3 per person a year; 18.9% are for professional reasons (70.9 million trips, 1.2 nights on average) (PDF p.16-17).
- Beyond 80 km the car is used for 72.4% of trips, the train for 13.8%, the plane for 9.4%, the coach for 2.5%; among people aged 19 to 24 the train reaches 25.5% of personal trips (PDF p.18-19).
- Method: official statistics survey run between May 2018 and April 2019 on 20,000 households representative of metropolitan France, people aged 6 and over; it also collected season tickets and motorway subscriptions (PDF p.8).

## Limits

Data collected in 2018-2019, before the pandemic, as the publication itself warns. Published as a summary: daily long-distance commuters are not separated from business trips, and nothing is given on trips of 30-80 km between cities, which fall inside 'local mobility'. One main means per trip, so legs are invisible. The detailed tables and microdata are available from the statistics office but were not downloaded. France only.
