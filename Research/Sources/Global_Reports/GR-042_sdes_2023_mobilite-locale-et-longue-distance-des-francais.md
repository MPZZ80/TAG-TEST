## What it says

A full picture of how the French travel, locally (within 80 km of home) and over long distances (more than 80 km), which is close to the intercity commute.

## Data and quotes used

- "En 2019, les Français réalisent trois déplacements (dans un rayon de 80 km autour de leur domicile) par jour en semaine. Ils consacrent 1 h 02 à se déplacer, soit 6 minutes de plus qu'en 2008." In English: in 2019 the French make three trips a day on weekdays within 80 km of home and spend 1 h 02 travelling, 6 minutes more than in 2008 (web page summary; working translation).
- The car remains the first mode of transport and accounts for 63% of trips; outside urban units it is used for four trips out of five (web page summary; working translation).

## Limits

The data is from 2018 and 2019, before the pandemic. The 22-page publication and the data tables were not read beyond the contents and definitions; the commuting and long-distance figures are still to be extracted.
