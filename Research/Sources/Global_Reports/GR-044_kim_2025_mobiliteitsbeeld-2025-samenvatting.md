## What it says

Overview of Dutch mobility in 2023 and 2024, with attention to train, bus, tram and metro, aviation and the extent of working from home.

## Data and quotes used

- The total distance travelled by residents aged 6 and over on Dutch territory was 4% lower in 2023 than in 2014, on average 11,300 km per person per year, 31 km per person per day (PDF p.2: "4% lager dan in 2014 ... gemiddeld 11.300 km per persoon per jaar"; working translation).
