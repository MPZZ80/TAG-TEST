## What it says

The results of the 2024 mobility survey of the Madrid region, a telephone survey of residents aged 14 to 80 about the trips of the previous day, by the regional transport authority. It gives the modal split, the number of legs per trip, the flows between the city and the outer rings, the fare products used, and work from home by ring of residence, with a comparison to the 2018 household survey. It is the only source collected that reports legs per trip and telework by distance from the centre in one place.

## Data and quotes used

- Work and education are the main purpose of 41.5% of trips (PDF p.49, figure 19: "Trabajo y formación 41,5%").
- The survey recorded more than 14.4 million trips and almost 16 million stages, an average of 1.10 stages per trip (PDF p.40: "más de 14,4 millones de viajes y casi 16 millones de etapas, con un promedio general de 1,10 etapas por viaje"; working translation; the period covered is not checked).
- 91.3% of trips in the Madrid region have one leg only; 7.3% have two legs and 1.4% three. Almost all trips with several legs are public transport trips; trips by private vehicle have 1.01 legs on average, public transport trips 1.36 (PDF p.42-43).
- Combining private vehicle and other means is negligible: only 0.1% of all trips are two-leg trips whose main means is the private vehicle (table 22, PDF p.43).
- Modal split of trips: private vehicle 36.8%, on foot 32.8%, public transport 27.6%, other 2.8%. Against 2018 the private vehicle lost 4 points (PDF p.41-42, p.78).
- The longer the trip, the more it is made by public transport: public transport takes 2.4% of trips under 10 minutes, 41.2% of trips of 31-45 minutes, 53.0% of 46-60 minutes and 70.9% of 61-90 minutes; the private vehicle takes 15.8% of trips of 61-90 minutes (table 23, PDF p.44).
- 90% of public transport trips are made with the season pass, which is valid on all means of the regional system; 7% with a 10-trip ticket, 2.2% with a single ticket (PDF p.45).
- Work from home: 17.8% of employed people telework at least one day a week. In the outer regional ring 41.5% of teleworkers do so five days a week, against 20.3% in central Madrid, where one or two days prevail; the report reads telework in the outer ring as 'a stable solution to avoid long trips, more than an occasional practice' (PDF p.54-55; working translation).
- Telework by day: Monday and Friday are slightly above the others (about 21% each) and Thursday is lowest (16.9%), so presence concentrates from Tuesday to Thursday (PDF p.55).
- The outer regional ring grew most in population between 2018 and 2024 (+15.7%, against +1.2% in central Madrid), which the report links to the search for affordable housing and to telework (PDF p.75).
- Work trips from the outer regional ring go mostly to the metropolitan ring and Madrid: only 1.5% of all work trips are internal to the regional ring, against 4.8% that link it to the other rings (table 31, PDF p.52).
- Trips per person fell 3.4% between 2018 and 2024 (2.6 to 2.5 a day), which the report links to telework and online study (PDF p.76).
- Households without a car make 50.4% of their trips by public transport; households with two cars 16.2% and with three or more 12.1% (table 21, PDF p.42).
- Drivers park on the street at destination in 41% of trips and in private parking in 39%; paid public car parks are used in under 1% (PDF p.46).
- Free or discounted passes in force in 2024 show in the data: people aged 26-45 raised their public transport share from 22.8% to 29.3%, while those aged 46-64 did not change (PDF p.78-79).

## Limits

One region of Spain with a dense, integrated public transport system and a single pass, so the high public transport share on long trips and the low count of mixed trips do not transfer to regions without that integration. Telephone survey of one day's trips; the sample size and margins of error are in the method chapters, which were not read. Legs are counted by change of means or line; walking to a stop is not a leg. Trips are inside one region: flows between Madrid and other provinces appear only as 3,140 trips 'outside the region'. Some tables came through the conversion with merged columns and should be checked in the PDF before quoting.
