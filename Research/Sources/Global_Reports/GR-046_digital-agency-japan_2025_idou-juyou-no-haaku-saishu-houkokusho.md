## What it says

Classifies Japanese municipalities by population density and by dependence on public transport for commuting and school, using the 2020 national census.

## Data and quotes used

- The report sorts regions into four types: large cities, suburban cities, regional cities and hilly or mountainous areas, by population density and by the share of commuters and students who depend on public transport (PDF p.16; working translation of the figure labels).

## Limits

Written in Japanese, so every statement is my working translation and should be checked by a Japanese reader. 24 of 227 pages have little text; OCR added the text of 5 of them and found nothing on the others, which are divider pages. Chart labels inside text pages may still be missing, so check figures in the original. Focus is on demand for new services, not on intercity commuting.
