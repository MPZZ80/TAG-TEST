## What it says

Analysis of the state of regional transport in Japan and of MaaS projects, and the measures proposed to promote MaaS 2.0. It states that some demonstration projects end when subsidies end, probably because of service quality problems, slow growth of users and lack of profitability.

## Data and quotes used

- Of the MaaS-related projects between 2019 and 2024, about 43% ended as demonstrations without reaching operation, as of October 2024 (PDF p.17: "2019年～2024年の間で運用まで至らず実証終了したMaaS関連事業は約43%（2024年10月現在）"; working translation).
- Some demonstration projects end when subsidies and other support stop; likely causes are service quality problems, users that grow slowly and a lack of profitability (PDF p.17; working translation).

## Limits

Written in Japanese; quotes are the original with my working translation. This is the document behind the figure that a web summary had reported without a source. The ministry also publishes it to justify its policy. Only a few pages were read, out of 164.
