## What it says

Announces a public-private pilot for a national MaaS service: a data hub where public bodies share data on the different modes (called open MaaS), and private platform operators that launch a national MaaS app within the year. The text says that today people check the route on the phone and then book and pay in a separate app for each mode.

## Data and quotes used

- Quote: "현재는 스마트폰으로 교통수단과 최적경로를 확인한 뒤 각각의 앱을 통해 본인이 원하는 교통수단을 예약하고 결제하는 것이 일반적이다." In English: today it is usual to check the modes and best route on the phone, and then book and pay for each mode in its own app (working translation).
- The hub aims to gather and share data on the various modes in one place so that data standards and a base system can be built (article text; working translation).

## Limits

An announcement of plans from January 2023, not results. A web summary reported results of a Daegu trial (35% less commuting time, 93% satisfaction); that is not in this article and is not verified. Written in Korean.
