## What it says

Announces a public-private pilot for a national MaaS service: a data hub where public bodies share data on the different modes (called open MaaS), and private platform operators that launch a national MaaS app within the year. The text says that today people check the route on the phone and then book and pay in a separate app for each mode.

## Data and quotes used

- Quote: "현재는 스마트폰으로 교통수단과 최적경로를 확인한 뒤 각각의 앱을 통해 본인이 원하는 교통수단을 예약하고 결제하는 것이 일반적이다." In English: today it is usual to check the modes and best route on the phone, and then book and pay for each mode in its own app (working translation).
- The hub aims to gather and share data on the various modes in one place so that data standards and a base system can be built (article text; working translation).
- Government policy news of 18 January 2023: the Metropolitan Transport Commission of the Korean transport ministry starts a public-private pilot for a nationwide MaaS: one app to book and pay 'from taxi to bus, rail and air', with a nationwide app planned within 2023 (web page, captured 2026-10-06).
- How people do it today, in the ministry's words, KO: '현재는 스마트폰으로 교통수단과 최적경로를 확인한 뒤 각각의 앱을 통해 본인이 원하는 교통수단을 예약하고 결제하는 것이 일반적이다' (EN: at present it is usual to check the means of transport and the best route on the smartphone and then book and pay for each means in its own app) (web page).
- What the ministry sees as the core: KO: 'MaaS 서비스의 실현을 위해서는 여러 교통수단 간의 데이터 연계가 핵심이다' (EN: linking data between the different means of transport is the key to making MaaS real). Plan: a public body is selected as intermediary to build and run an 'open MaaS' base with data standards; private platform operators are then selected to build the app on it (web page).
- Expected by the ministry: flat-rate and subscription products once the nationwide service works, and links with tourism and lodging (web page).
