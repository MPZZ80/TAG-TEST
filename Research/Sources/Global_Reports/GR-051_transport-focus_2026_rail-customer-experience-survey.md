## What it says

Satisfaction of rail passengers in Great Britain with the train, punctuality, frequency and how delays are handled.

## Data and quotes used

- Among passengers who were delayed on their journey, less than half were satisfied with punctuality (45 per cent) and 48 per cent were satisfied with how well the delay was handled (page text).
- Commuters were less likely to be satisfied with how the delay was handled (41 per cent) than those travelling for leisure (55 per cent) (page text).
- Overall, 87 per cent of passengers were satisfied with the train they were travelling on; satisfaction with the frequency of trains on the route was lower, at 76 per cent (page text).

## Limits
