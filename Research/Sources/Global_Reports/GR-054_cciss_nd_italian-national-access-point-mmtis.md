## What it says

Explains that the national access point gathers data on different modes of transport through regional access points, in the NeTEx standard with an Italian profile, following the EU delegated regulation 2017/1926.

## Data and quotes used

- "The National Access Point for Multimodal Mobility gathers information and data on different modes of transport, with the aim of facilitating the consultation of data and enabling their dissemination through a single portal, according to the European Delegated Regulation 1926/2017."
- Static data is provided by transport authorities, operators and infrastructure managers through Regional Access Points using the European standard NeTEx CEN/TS 16614 and its Italian profile (page text).

## Limits

A description of the portal, not an evaluation of how complete or used the data is. A web summary said 144 parties supply information; that is not on the page read and is not verified.
