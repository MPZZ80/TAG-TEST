## What it says

Twelve years of travel diaries on a German railway enthusiasts' forum, most of them by one writer who first studies and then works, travels regularly between the same cities (Munich and Dresden, later Basel and southern Germany) and logs every trip. Read in full, the thread shows how an experienced traveller copes with a network of tight connections: he keeps his own statistics of connections made, follows personal rules (never the last connection of the day, a charged phone, a known fallback route, a place at the right door), checks several information channels that often contradict each other and trusts none of them fully, and asks staff to phone ahead so that the next train waits. The first and last legs (city bus, tram, underground, replacement bus, a lift by car) are where minutes are lost and where no one protects the connection. Other passengers in the diaries react to the same events by phoning home, asking strangers, being fetched by car or saying that next time they will drive.

## Data and quotes used

- The forum describes the thread as the place for readers' reports and travel accounts ("Eure Reportagen und Reiseberichte finden hier ihren Platz"; in English: your reports and travel accounts find their place here; working translation).
- FIRST READING (page 4, posts of May to September 2016). German railway forum, section for travel reports; page 4 of a long thread, written in 2016 by a handful of frequent rail travellers (thread page, captured 2026-10-07).
- A missed last connection turns a small delay into a big one: a traveller arrives in Munich 100 minutes late after a problem in Austria and reaches home about 120 minutes late because 'die letzte RB ab Stuttgart Hbf (00:15) nach Heilbronn halt keinen sinnvollen FV-Anschluss von Muenchen' has (EN: the last regional train from Stuttgart has no usable long-distance connection from Munich) (post of 22 May 2016).
- A 3-minute connection decides an 8-hour trip: the writer waits to buy the ticket hoping the official 3-minute connection is added to the planner; it is not, so the planned trip Munich-Dresden takes almost eight hours. A signal-box fault gives +24 minutes and 'dass ich meinen ausserplanmaessigen Anschluss vergessen kann' (EN: I can forget my unofficial connection). Final arrival +175 minutes, after 1 am (post of 15 June 2016).
- The planner is optimistic during a delay: 'Waehrend die Fahrplanauskunft optimistisch von einer Verspaetungsreduktion auf +17 bis Hof ausgeht, ist das Gegenteil der Fall' (EN: while the journey planner optimistically expects the delay to shrink to +17, the opposite happens): +30, +33, +40 (post of 15 June 2016).
- No information at the station: the train disappears from the platform display, 'hinterlaesst ratlose Fahrgaeste' (EN: leaves passengers at a loss); the driver and conductor 'sind genauso ratlos wie die ratsuchenden Fahrgaeste' (EN: are as lost as the passengers); the driver phones the control room and understands 15 minutes when 50 were said; the display later shows only a blue screen (post of 15 June 2016).
- Late information removes the alternative: after the announcement of a one-hour wait an older man shouts 'WAS SAGT DER DAS NICHT FRUEHER ... DANN HAETTE ICH JA UEBER GERA FAHREN KOENNEN' (EN: why did he not say so earlier, I could have gone via Gera), while the train to Gera is just leaving. Another passenger: 'Sagen Sie doch gleich, dass nichts mehr kommt. Dann wissen wir wenigstens Bescheid' (EN: tell us straight away that nothing is coming, then at least we know) (post of 15 June 2016).
- Effect on choice: a student who normally uses ride sharing took the train for a tax reason; on the way out a big delay forced him to take a taxi to arrive on time, at his own cost: 'Eigentlich habe er nach den beiden Fahrten ueberhaupt keine Lust mehr, mit der Bahn zu fahren' (EN: after the two trips he has no wish at all to take the train again). The writer agrees: last summer he never arrived with less than +30 (post of 15 June 2016).
- Passenger rights do not cover it: the regional day ticket gives nothing back, and the taxi is not refunded (post of 15 June 2016).
- Planner results people do not believe: a passenger is surprised that a trip with three changes is faster than the direct one; his partner notes 'das sind nur ganz kurze Umsteigezeiten ... nur 5 Minuten' (EN: those are very short changes, only 5 minutes) (post of 5 August 2016).
- Works on the line make a planned connection miss by three minutes: 50 minutes of waiting at the junction; the trip of 640 km takes over nine hours, chosen for the price (post of 5 August 2016).
- Last urban leg, Munich: with 11 minutes of planned change the writer tries a 1-minute one and misses the tram by 20 seconds; a stop display shows the next bus in 23 minutes while the one in the shelter shows 1 minute, and he bets on a display error (post of 7 August 2016). 'natuerlich faehrt die naechste Tram in 15 Minuten' at the end of a 6-hour trip (post of 4 July 2016).
- Travel time is used and valued: writers study, work on a paper or look out; one calls 6 hours 'angenehme' (EN: pleasant) but is 'nach 7 Uhr nicht mehr aufnahmefaehig' (EN: unable to take anything in after 7 pm) (posts of 4 July and 5 August 2016). The signature of one writer keeps yearly statistics of his own trips: 30,600 km, connections reached 89.1%, punctuality within 6 minutes in Germany 57.8%.
- Small frictions with tickets: no working ticket machine at a station, a queue too long to buy before departure; staff let people board and pay later (posts of 14 June 2016).
- SECOND READING (pages 1 to 3 and 5 to 10). Posts are cited by date. All quotes are German originals with a working English translation.
- The main writer keeps his own records. Twelve trips on a route with two 3-minute transfers, the last connection of the day: all trains on time 1; the connection waited 9 times; another route after losing it 1; a night stranded at the junction 1 (post of 6 January 2015). At his transfer station since October 2013, mostly transfers of 3 to 7 minutes: 77 of 80 made, 96.2% ('Der Knoten Hof ist also deutlich besser als sein Ruf'; EN: the node is clearly better than its reputation) (26 September 2016). First half of 2020: 'durchschnittlich 4,7% Reisezeitverlängerung' and '80,6% Anschlusserreichquote sind mit Abstand der niedrigste Wert seit Beginn der Statistik im Jahr 2014' (EN: on average 4.7% longer travel time; 80.6% of connections made is by far the lowest value since his statistics began in 2014) (3 September 2020). By 2019 he had logged 568 trips (11 September 2019). A bad trip is 'wohl so auf jede zehnte bis fünfzehnte Fahrt' (EN: about every tenth to fifteenth trip) (14 November 2021). He writes up only the trips where something happens (22 May 2014).
- Personal rules stated by writers and readers: 'nach Möglichkeit nicht die letztmögliche Verbindung des Tages zu nehmen und grundsätzlich immer mit vollem Handy unterwegs zu sein' (EN: if possible not the last connection of the day, and always travel with a full phone) (20 June 2014); a power bank (June 2014); 'Alles unter 10 Min. Übergangszeit ist für mich kein Anschluss, bzw. reine Papieranschlüsse' (EN: anything under 10 minutes of transfer time is no connection for me, only a connection on paper) (3 September 2020); 'nur durchgehende Reiseketten buche und/oder genug Puffer einbaue' (EN: I book only through journeys and/or build in enough buffer) (19 September 2017); the writer's rule 'keine Verspätungsmeldung, bevor der Anschluss nicht wirklich weg ist' (EN: do not tell anyone you will be late before the connection has really gone) (8 July 2022); a later train kept as 'Meine eiserne Reserve' (EN: my iron reserve) (23 December 2016).
- What he does on every trip: gets an e-mail from the railway's delay alert before leaving and re-checks the delay several times during the day (6 January 2015); checks the departure from work and weighs leaving 20 minutes earlier (25 December 2017); checks the onward connection on the phone before arriving and works out the alternatives himself ('Sicherheitshalber kontrolliere ich schon mal die Alternativen'; EN: to be safe I already check the alternatives) (24 June 2017, 10 August 2015); stands at the door nearest the stairs and misses a train by half a minute when he picks the wrong end (25 December 2017); asks the conductor to phone ahead so that the connection waits ('ich bitte um Vormeldung meines Anschlusses') (4 November 2015, 11 September 2019); phones the railway's station service centre, a tip he took from another passenger (2 August 2015, 13 March 2021).
- Forecasts are not trusted. '+25 wegen Personen im Gleis ... Soll ich darauf vertrauen ...? Lieber nicht. Zu oft habe ich schon erlebt, dass die Verspätung deutlich geringer oder gar nicht vorhanden war' (EN: should I trust it? Better not. Too often the delay was much smaller or not there at all), and the train comes almost on time (10 July 2015). 'der DB Navigator verheißt nichts Gutes ... Aber die Daten sind ja bekanntlich nicht so ganz verlässlich' (EN: the app promises nothing good, but its data is known to be not entirely reliable) (24 June 2017). The app shows a connecting train on time while the train it must wait for on the single track is shown 6 minutes late: 'Das ist nun wirklich einfache Logik ... Das schafft die tolle Verspätungsprognose im DB Navigator aber offenbar nicht' (EN: that is simple logic, which the delay forecast apparently cannot manage) (3 November 2022). A forecast of +45 stays until the train arrives with +25 and surprises the people waiting (27 December 2024). Announced delays that grow step by step: +10, +15, +20, +23, +32, +36, with staff saying 'das sind Circa-Angaben' (EN: these are approximate figures) (4 January 2019).
- Channels contradict each other. The platform display says +40 while 'laut DB Navigator ist der Zug pünktlich weitergefahren' (EN: according to the app the train left on time); one conductor tells people to get off and another to get on; the writer's ironic rule is 'Sorge stets für widersprüchliche Informationen auf allen Kanälen' (EN: always provide contradictory information on all channels); staff of another operator say 'Wir benutzen nicht dieselben Apps und Informationskanäle wie die DB' (EN: we do not use the same apps and information channels as the national railway) and send passengers to a train that is in no app (15 October 2018). A replacement train exists but 'Der DB Navigator verkündet nur, dass der ECE ausfällt, weiß aber nichts von der Ersatzfahrt' (EN: the app only says the train is cancelled and knows nothing of the replacement); the Swiss railway's app proposes the onward chain that the German one does not (14 November 2021). An operator's own website listed nothing the day before a cut-short service that the app showed as cancelled (1 January 2026).
- Staff often know no more than passengers. 'Meine Damen und Herren, wir wissen selbst nicht so genau, wo wir wann wohin fahren' (EN: ladies and gentlemen, we do not quite know ourselves where we are going and when) (4 November 2015). 'Wir haben leider keine Informationen erhalten, ob die RB 74 nach Tübingen auf uns wartet ... Sie werden es dann sehen' (EN: we have received no information on whether the connecting train will wait; you will see) (8 July 2022). A conductor's device has no signal: 'Fehler beim Laden. Ahso, ich hab kein Netz' (20 June 2014). The stated cause is often 'Verzögerungen im Betriebsablauf' (EN: delays in operations): 'wir haben Verspätung, weil wir Verspätung haben' (EN: we are late because we are late) (31 December 2021). The writer also records good cases: conductors who tell every passenger in person, repeat announcements in English and hold a connection (11 September 2019, 14 November 2021, 19 September 2024).
- Late information removes the alternative: a fault is announced only 'NACHDEM am Gleis gegenüber der IC weggefahren ist, den ich ... hätte nehmen können' (EN: after the train on the opposite platform, which I could have taken, had left) (23 December 2016). At a junction he has six minutes to decide, the information desk has a queue of ten and the travel centre asks him to take a number; staff: 'Das müssen Sie entscheiden' (EN: you have to decide that) (20 June 2014).
- The journey planner hides or distorts options. After a timetable change it stops showing his usual connection because the minimum transfer time at the station was raised from 3 to 4 minutes, and offers a route 1 hour 46 longer; the trains still meet at the same platform and 'Ich werde also Zeuge davon, wie 15 Fahrgäste einen nicht existenten Umstieg durchführen' (EN: I watch 15 passengers make a transfer that does not exist) (6 January 2015). For a cross-border regional trip 'Alleine die Verbindung herauszusuchen, war schon eine große Herausforderung' (EN: just finding the connection was a big challenge): the planner keeps proposing a coach he cannot exclude and the Czech planner finds nothing until he raises its default limit of four changes; no through fare exists, so he splits the ticket where he expects the 3-minute transfer to fail (11 September 2019). Fares exist via one route and not another (12 September 2019); half an hour of trying to get a cheap fare (5 June 2019).
- Price decides the route and the risk: an all-regional route for 17.90 euro against 64 euro for the fastest (2 September 2020); a route 2.5 hours longer because it costs 25.40 euro (16 October 2016); an 8-minute transfer refused as 'zu riskant' (EN: too risky) and the earlier option refused as too expensive (1 November 2022).
- First mile. Relatives offer the car as backup: 'wenn der Bus nicht kommt, dann rufst du an und ich fahre dich mit dem Auto' (EN: if the bus does not come, call and I will drive you) (2 August 2015). City bus to the station with 8 minutes of transfer: in steady rain the bus fills, loses 8 minutes, 'Ich checke meine Zugabfahrt, steht leider pünktlich drin' (EN: I check my train, unfortunately shown on time), and he misses it by three minutes; a new full-price ticket costs 71.75 euro. He notes that in the rain 'Die Radfahrer sind nicht nur aufs Auto, sondern auch auf den ÖV umgestiegen' (EN: the cyclists have switched not only to the car but also to public transport) (31 May 2021). A city bus caught in football traffic reaches +32 and costs him an hour; next time he avoids that bus line (1 and 3 November 2022). A local train to the main station is cancelled though nothing was forecast before he left home; buses of other operators are not on the real-time displays (18 September 2016). A writer who did not re-check online before leaving home calls it 'den ersten Fehler' (EN: the first mistake) (19 September 2024).
- Last mile. Train +14 with a 6-minute transfer to a bus: the bus is itself +11. 'Merke: Anschlusssicherung in Deutschland bedeutet meistens, darauf zu hoffen, dass der Anschluss mehr Verspätung hat als der Zubringer' (EN: note: protecting a connection in Germany mostly means hoping that the connection is later than the feeder) (3 January 2020). After midnight: a 60-second run to the last bus, whose driver reopens the door (24 June 2017); tram in 23 minutes at 00:51 (2 August 2015); a transfer missed means 2 km on foot or a 20-minute wait (3 January 2020, 31 December 2021). A town where long-distance trains use the outer station and the link to the centre misses by minutes; a reader walked 20 minutes along a road, another answers 'E-Roller kaufen' (EN: buy an e-scooter) (1 to 4 August 2021). A reader's workaround for a 27-minute wait between train and city bus: take the bus in the opposite direction and change to a tram, 'sozusagen durch die Hintertür' (EN: through the back door, so to speak) (2 September 2020). A boat is not covered by rail passenger rights, so a suspended ferry breaks the chain (1 April 2023).
- Separate tickets leave the risk with the traveller. An airport bus and a train-specific ticket bought separately: 'Keine durchgehende Reisekette, keine durchgehenden Fahrgastrechte ... In dem Fall trägt der Kunde alleine das Wegerisiko' (EN: no through journey, no through passenger rights; in that case the customer alone carries the risk of the way); the two could once be booked together and 'Die Kooperation mit der Bahn besteht momentan nicht' (EN: the cooperation with the railway does not exist at present) (19 and 20 September 2017). A passenger rebooked onto another operator asks whether her ticket is valid (5 May 2015); a private operator's train is the next way out but 'da gilt meine Fahrkarte nicht' (EN: my ticket is not valid there) (21 May 2016). In Italy, after a breakdown, stranded passengers are told they may use other trains only by buying another ticket (12 September 2026).
- Replacement buses are the weakest link. 'Anscheinend weiß niemand so genau, welcher Bus nun wann wohin fährt' (EN: apparently nobody knows which bus goes where and when); about 50 people at a small station at 22:45 with a display showing only the cancelled train (2 August 2015). Two replacement buses, one direct and one calling everywhere, are not labelled and most passengers board the slow one; 'ein Hinweis "Express" oder "hält überall" wäre klar verständlich' (EN: a sign saying express or all stops would be easy to understand); a passenger asks 'Why can't they tell us on Google Maps or Apple that our fucking train is not running?' (8 July 2022). Neither the departure boards nor the app knew of a replacement bus; one articulated bus for a full five-coach train (14 November 2021). The operator plans transfer times so that 'man letztendlich an beiden Enden immer den Anschluss knapp verpasst' (EN: in the end you just miss the connection at both ends) (August 2014). Train and replacement bus 'nicht miteinander reden können' (EN: cannot talk to each other) (22 August 2022). Before boarding he asks the driver, 'um nicht versehentlich wieder den langsamen ... zu erwischen' (EN: so as not to catch the slow one again) (3 November 2022).
- What other passengers do when a connection is at risk: they phone ahead and are asked for 'eine Bestätigung von der Bahn' (EN: a confirmation from the railway) for school or employer (7 April 2014, 2 August 2015); they call relatives for a lift, and teenagers ask 'ab wann macht es Sinn, sich abholen zu lassen?' (EN: from what point does it make sense to be fetched?) (2 August 2015, 8 July 2022); a woman cancels her lift on hearing the connection is lost, the train waits after all, and she cannot reach the driver again (8 July 2022); a phone battery dies and the number for the lift is lost with it (2 August 2015); colleagues cannot load the tram route to their destination before arriving ('Hmm, das lädt nicht') (30 December 2020); mobile dead zones recur (2014 to 2023). Infrequent riders board the wrong train or the wrong portion of a train (4 August 2014, 5 April 2016, 27 December 2024).
- Statements about switching to the car, all overheard or reported: 'nächstes mal fahre ich bestimmt wieder mit dem Auto' (EN: next time I will certainly drive again) (August 2014); 'Wenn wir jetzt aussteigen könnten und ein Auto nähmen, wären wir in anderthalb Stunden da' (EN: if we could get off now and take a car we would be there in an hour and a half) (10 July 2015); 'Mit dem Auto wäre ich viel schneller gewesen und billiger ist es auch noch' (EN: by car I would have been much faster and it is cheaper too) (5 June 2019). A weekend commuter by rail for over seven years ends it: 'nach über 7 Jahren habe ich die Schnauze gestrichen voll' (EN: after more than seven years I have had enough) (5 August 2014). The main writer says he has no alternative to the train (22 May 2014).
- Commuting cases overheard. A woman who drives to the station: since she starts later she takes the 8:17 train and 'da finde ich immer nur sehr schwierig einen Parkplatz' (EN: then it is always very hard to find a parking space); asked about cycling to the station she refuses because of the hill on the way back; 'wenn ich keinen Parkplatz finde, muss ich halt doch wieder früher fahren' (EN: if I find no parking space I will have to leave earlier again) (1 January 2026). A man who lives in Cologne and works in Karlsruhe: 'ich fahre nicht jeden Tag ... und meistens aus dem Homeoffice arbeiten' (EN: I do not travel every day and mostly work from home) (31 May 2021). Cross-border commuters squeezed into a train of half the usual length (31 May 2021). A driver stopped three times for other trains: 'da sind lauter Pendler in meinem Zug, die auf dem Weg zur Arbeit sind' (EN: my train is full of commuters on their way to work) (1 January 2023). A colleague who commutes daily by car, 40 minutes of country road after the motorway (5 June 2019).
- Getting around the station: a footbridge from which the platform displays cannot be seen (11 September 2019); an unannounced platform change that turns a 4-minute transfer into a run through the underpass, platform numbers out of order (8 July 2022); stairs unlit for two months and no lift, a wheelchair user must ride past and come back (8 July and 1 November 2022); a lift too small for a bicycle (2 January 2024); platforms too short, so half a train runs locked (15 July 2022).
- Passenger rights in practice: a taxi promised by the conductor is first refused at the information desk; arrival after 2 am, more than 180 minutes late; the claim form is unclear ('War das jetzt eine abgebrochene Fahrt? Oder eine andere Reiseroute?'; EN: was that an abandoned journey, or another route?) and pays 18.40 euro (20 June 2014). A taxi voucher for the last 50 km issued in two minutes (21 May 2016). A hotel voucher offered to a mother with two children who misses the last train: 'Den privaten Teil müssen Sie selbst regeln' (EN: you have to sort out the private part yourself) (2 January 2024). Fare walls between train categories and regional tickets are debated at length by readers (16 to 25 August 2022).
- The writer's own summing-up: 'In der Schweiz ist gute Planung alles, in Deutschland ist das Gelingen vor allem Glückssache. Wie man es so oft von leidgeplagten Fahrgästen hört: "Irgendwas ist immer."' (EN: in Switzerland good planning is everything, in Germany success is above all a matter of luck; as one so often hears from long-suffering passengers, there is always something), and yet the trains are full (1 January 2026). 'Anschlussroulette' (EN: connection roulette) is his word for a route with a 9-minute and a 4-minute transfer that he rode five times in 2022, making the connection by luck when the other train was late too (8 July and 3 November 2022). A reader on 20 minutes of delay: 'das üblich gewordene Verspätungs- und Ausfall-Lotto' (EN: the delay and cancellation lottery that has become usual) (December 2016).

## Limits

A railway enthusiasts' forum, grade C: what it reports are the experiences and perceptions of a few people, not measurements. Most of the long posts are by one writer, a student and later an employee, who travels far more than an average traveller, enjoys the trip, knows the rules and chooses cheap regional routes with tight transfers on purpose; he says himself that he writes up only the trips where something happens. His trips are mostly long regional and long-distance journeys of 6 to 10 hours at weekends and holidays, in Germany, Czechia, Austria and Switzerland; he is not a daily intercity commuter, and the commuters in the thread appear only in overheard conversations. His personal statistics are his own counts on his own routes. A large part of the thread is unrelated to the project (overheard private conversations, rolling stock, scenery) and was read and left out. The mechanics it shows (tight connections, the first and last leg, contradictory information, what people do when a connection is at risk) apply to any chain of services; how often they occur cannot be taken from it. Translations are working translations; quotes were shortened with ellipses, so check the original post by its date before reuse. Nicknames were not copied; where a quote contained the writer's nickname it was left out.
