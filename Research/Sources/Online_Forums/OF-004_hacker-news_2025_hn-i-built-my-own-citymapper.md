## Data and quotes used

- Thread of 2025-10-27 with 21 comments (API count).
- Forum thread on a technology news site about a developer who built his own trip planner for London; 21 comments, mostly by developers (thread, captured 2026-10-07).
- Open tools and data exist to build a trip planner: OneBusAway (used in several cities), OpenTripPlanner ('the biggest deployment being ENTUR's in Norway, which covers the entire country. I believe all trip planning apps in Norway use this deployment'), GTFS feeds collected in a public database, the London transport API (thread).
- National rail is the hard part: a developer asks for a free journey planner API for UK national rail and finds the official one 'not free so automatically outside of my app development budget' (thread).
- The limit is the service, not the app: 'half the time TFL seems to completely ignore its own bus schedule ... You rush for a bus and it leaves 2mins before schedule ... I would rather longer waits and better predictability' (thread).
- Another user: a bus 'so unreliable I'd rather walk 20 minutes in the sub zero temperatures - was supposed to run every ten minutes, but in reality there was no bus for an hour and then 5 in quick succession' (thread).

Useful only for two points: open data and engines exist where authorities publish them (Norway as a whole country), and predictability is preferred to speed by the two users who speak as travellers.
