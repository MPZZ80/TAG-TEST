## What it says

A sample of 100 Italian App Store reviews of Google Maps. Almost all are about driving: routes that people find longer or strange, the app changing route by itself, low voice volume, old maps. Only two of the 100 mention public transport. A fifth of the sample is a protest about place names and several are reviews of shops posted by mistake, so the star ratings say little about the product.

## Data and quotes used

- Store rating on 2026-10-07: 4.64 average from 517,759 ratings in the Italy store; app version 26.40.3, released 2026-10-02 (Apple lookup).
- Sample: 100 reviews, 5 stars: 20, 4 stars: 9, 3 stars: 5, 2 stars: 10, 1 stars: 56.
- Sample: 100 reviews, App Store Italy, 2026-08-15 to 2026-10-04; ratings: 56 one-star, 10 two-star, 5 three-star, 9 four-star, 20 five-star (store sample). ## Limits

App store reviews over-represent anger and are prompted by updates; one-star reviews are 56% here while the product is used by nearly everyone. A fifth of the sample is a political protest and a tenth is misplaced shop reviews. Reviews do not say who commutes or how far. Car navigation dominates, so the sample says almost nothing about combined trips. Counts by theme are my rough reading of 100 items, not market data. Translations are working translations.
