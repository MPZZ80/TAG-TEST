## What it says

A sample of 100 Italian App Store reviews of Moovit, split between very satisfied and very angry users. The two complaints are advertising that blocks the screen for seconds exactly when people are in a hurry, and bus arrival times that do not match reality. The praise is that it is precise and brings all local transport into one place. People judge it on one thing: whether the minutes to the next bus are true.

## Data and quotes used

- Store rating on 2026-10-07: 4.54 average from 49,128 ratings in the Italy store; app version 5.202.0, released 2026-10-05 (Apple lookup).
- Sample: 100 reviews, 5 stars: 49, 4 stars: 6, 3 stars: 3, 2 stars: 4, 1 stars: 38.
- Sample: 100 reviews, App Store Italy, 2026-07-14 to 2026-10-02; ratings: 49 five-star, 6 four-star, 3 three-star, 4 two-star, 38 one-star (store sample). My count by theme, approximate: about 22 complain about advertising; about 20 about wrong or unreliable times and routes; about 6 about the paid tier or billing; about 45 short praise.
- Advertising costs the bus: IT 'mi compare la pubblicita obbligatoria da 5/10 secondi non skippabile che mi fa perdere secondi preziosi, che possono anche determinare se prendo o perdo (e successo) un autobus' (EN: a compulsory 5-10 second ad that cannot be skipped makes me lose precious seconds, which can decide whether I catch or miss a bus; it has happened) (2026-09-08); 'Quando hai fretta ti fa perdere tempo' (2026-09-24); 'non riesco a guardare quale sia il cambio giusto da fare perche devo aspettare' (EN: I cannot see which change to make because I have to wait) (2026-07-28).
- Wrong times: IT 'Sull app mi diceva 5 minuti poi alla fermata c'era scritto 53' (EN: the app said 5 minutes, then at the stop it said 53) (2026-09-29); 'inizialmente mi da 9 minuti ... poi passano e mi dice 15 minuti' with the paid version (2026-08-13); 'Il numero di autobus su strada e la meta di quello rappresentato' (EN: the buses on the road are half of those shown) (2026-09-12).
- The app is blamed together with the service, Milan: IT 'Se l'applicazione mi propone itinerari con tram che non passano dalle fermate dove dice che dovrebbero passare o non mi comunica i ritardi - costanti - di Trenord, il viaggiatore ... non puo piu fare affidamento neanche sull'app, che dovrebbe essere un supporto aggiornato e attendibile della situazione in tempo reale' (EN: if the app proposes trams that do not pass where it says or does not tell me the constant delays of the regional trains, the traveller can no longer rely even on the app, which should be an up-to-date and reliable support in real time) (2026-09-13).
- Stale data and no way to report: routes changed over a week ago and not updated, 'non c'e neanche un modo per segnalare questa cosa a meno che non sei un utente pro' (2026-09-22); times that 'Non corrispondono a quelli ufficiali' in Sardinia (2026-08-24); wrong routes in Perugia (2026-09-02).
- Seeing the vehicle matters more than the timetable: 'da quando non e piu possibile vedere la posizione dei pullman (e sapere se arriveranno effettivamente!!!) e quasi inutile' (EN: since one can no longer see where the buses are, and know whether they will actually come, it is almost useless) (2026-09-27).
- Paying on top of the pass: the paid tier's cost 'va a sommarsi spesso all'abbonamento della compagnia di autobus o di treno' (EN: often adds to the bus or train company's pass) (2026-07-18); 'Why one should pay for the subscription to see live update when every time it says that updates are unavailable' (2026-09-03); a trial charged as a yearly subscription (2026-07-31).
- What satisfied users say: 'Prendo molto spesso i bus, ma prima consulto i vostri orari, cosi non aspetto tanto alle fermate' (EN: I check your times first so I do not wait long at stops) (2026-09-08); 'E molto apprezzabile il suo ruolo di hub per tutti i trasporti' (2026-09-06); 'good reminder for us commuters' (2026-09-25); now also shows how full the vehicle is (2026-08-09).
- Speed is part of the job: 'Mentre carica il bus e gia partito' (EN: while it loads, the bus has already left) (2026-08-08).

## Limits

App store reviews over-represent strong feelings; many five-star reviews are two words and may be prompted by the app. Urban buses and trams in Italian cities (Rome, Milan, Florence, Naples); almost nothing on trips between cities. Wrong times may come from the operators' data, which the reviews cannot separate from the app. Counts by theme are my rough reading of 100 items, not market data. Translations are working translations.
