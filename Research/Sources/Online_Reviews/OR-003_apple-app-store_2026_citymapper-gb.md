## What it says

A sample of 100 UK App Store reviews of Citymapper, three quarters positive. Users praise it for making an unknown city usable and for being accurate; many use it abroad. The complaints are about recent loss of quality: longer or less direct routes, wrong first trains, platforms and walking times that made people miss a train, service problems missing from the departure board, and constant requests to accept advertising cookies.

## Data and quotes used

- Store rating on 2026-10-07: 4.80 average from 274,847 ratings in the United Kingdom store; app version 11.51.1, released 2026-09-24 (Apple lookup).
- Sample: 100 reviews, 5 stars: 67, 4 stars: 9, 3 stars: 2, 2 stars: 2, 1 stars: 20.
- Sample: 100 reviews, App Store United Kingdom, 2026-08-21 to 2026-10-04; ratings: 67 five-star, 9 four-star, 2 three-star, 2 two-star, 20 one-star (store sample). My count by theme, approximate: about 60 short praise; about 12 on wrong or worse routes and times; about 6 on the app running or tracking in the background; about 4 on cookies and privacy.
- The value named most: confidence in a place one does not know: 'Everyone is a local with this'; 'So useful for navigating unknown areas of London'; 'handy ... especially if you're abroad and have 0 idea of where you're going'; 'it tells you where to get off the bus too' (reviews of 2026-09-20, 09-16, 09-19, 08-21).
- Being on time: 'Citymapper is rarely wrong and my reputation for being late is transformed' (2026-09-08).
- Small errors in a change cost the train: 'Missed my train from King's Cross to Luton after running for 10 mins to catch it. App said 3 mins walk' (2026-09-04); 'dont trust platform data or youll miss your train' (2026-09-08); the app 'doesn't always get the connection times right when changing lines or platforms' (2026-09-23).
- Wrong first trains for early commuters: the app shows a 5:13 train from a station that opens at 5:20; it 'will send you to a closed station, causing you to miss the ghost train and then miss all other trains you needed to make to get to work' (2026-09-02); 'Keeps giving me wrong information which has delayed arrivals at work' (2026-08-31).
- A wanted function that was removed: 'Please bring back the ability to select the train either side of the suggested one (which also then updates the ETA) ... It was very useful to be able to see the knock on effect of getting either the earlier or later train' (2026-09-23).
- Disruption information: 'What use is a live departure board for nearby stations if it doesn't show service issues and line closures' (2026-09-03); 'Doesn't update roadworks, diversions' (2026-08-31).
- Routes felt to get worse: 'lately all journeys are the longer versions and indirect? A journey that is 40 mins becomes 60+ mins? Even friends have stopped using it' (2026-08-25); 'This app keeps adding long walking distances' (2026-09-18).
- Price shown with the alternatives is valued: 'the best part is that the price and other alternatives are clearly shown' (2026-09-29).
- Privacy as the price of a free app: 'why do I have to sell my soul to the ... data harvesting companies to use this great app' (2026-09-18); a paying user is still asked about cookies at every start and 'will not be renewing' (2026-09-29).

## Limits

App store reviews; many five-star reviews are one or two words. London and tourists in European cities, not commuters between cities. The complaints cluster in recent weeks and may reflect one update. Counts by theme are my rough reading of 100 items, not market data.
