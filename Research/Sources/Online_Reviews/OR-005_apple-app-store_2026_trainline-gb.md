## What it says

A sample of 100 UK App Store reviews of Trainline, half of them one-star. Booking is called easy; the anger is about money and about being left alone when things go wrong: booking fees, confusing ticket names that make people buy the wrong ticket, lost discount cards, refunds that are hard to get, and no help or alternative when a train is cancelled or a connection is missed. A daily commuter asks for one simple thing the app lacks: a standing alert on the two trains he takes every day.

- Splitting tickets saves money and people do it by hand with other tools to save more (2026-09-14).

## Limits

App store reviews over-represent anger; half the sample is one-star. Great Britain: a fare system far more complex than Italy's, so the ticket confusion is partly local; fees also are a British issue. Train only. Several complaints are about the railway, not the app. Counts by theme are my rough reading of 100 items, not market data.
