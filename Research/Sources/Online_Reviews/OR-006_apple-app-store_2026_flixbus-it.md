## What it says

A sample of 100 Italian App Store reviews of FlixBus over 15 months. The app itself is praised as quick and clear, with live tracking of the bus. The anger is about the service: long delays, cancelled or changed runs, refunds that are small or hard to obtain, and nobody to talk to. Several people had to buy a second ticket or a taxi to arrive. Delays make any onward connection impossible, and wrong information about delays or the departure point made people miss the bus.

- Sample: 100 reviews, App Store Italy, 2025-07-11 to 2026-09-30; ratings: 37 five-star, 5 four-star, 6 three-star, 4 two-star, 48 one-star (store sample). - Delay information that is wrong the other way: 'mi manda una mail avvisandomi di 40 minuti di ritardo. Abitando piuttosto lontano dal punto di ritrovo, mi organizzo per partire piu tardi. - The boarding point: the app's map showed bay 12 at the airport, the bus left from bay 1; the traveller missed it and then the onward bus to Bologna (2025-10-11); a station named differently from what people know made another miss the bus (2025-12-29); drivers could not say where the bus leaves from (2025-11-26).
- Changes decided by the company: a same-day cancellation by e-mail with a new ticket four hours later (2026-05-29); route changed the evening before, with a supplement to change (2025-08-08); a line withdrawn: 'Sembra un giocattolo, che viene messa ed eliminata a piacimento' (2025-10-22).
- Refunds: a ticket bought seconds before learning of a 70-minute delay, then 'un inferno burocratico' to withdraw within 60 minutes (2026-08-14); 10 euros back on a 50-euro ticket (2026-01-02); no compensation for delays under two hours (2025-08-25); a voucher valid five days in place of a refund (2026-06-05).
- The other side, a satisfied traveller comparing with the train: 'se il bus e in ritardo non importa, ti porta comunque a destinazione a differenza dei treni che perdi coincidenze e spendi molto di meno facendo un viaggio unico senza mille cambi' (EN: if the bus is late it does not matter, it still takes you to the destination, unlike trains where you miss connections; and you spend much less with one trip and no changes) (2025-08-20).
- What the app does well, by a critical user: buying, the ticket in the phone wallet, dates in the calendar, 'il tracciamento in tempo reale dell'autobus, la stima del ritardo ad ogni fermata della tratta, e un widget che aiuta a raggiungere la fermata' (EN: live tracking of the bus, estimated delay at each stop, and a widget that helps to reach the stop) (2026-09-11).
- Price moves while buying: doubled after the app closed (2026-09-08); 50 euros more in two minutes (2026-06-23).

## Limits

App store reviews; the sample spans 15 months, so it is thin per period. Occasional long-distance and tourist trips, including international ones, more than commuting; routes such as Milan-Turin and Genoa-Milan appear but no reviewer says they commute. Most complaints are about the bus service and staff, not the app. Counts by theme are my rough reading of 100 items, not market data. Translations are working translations.
