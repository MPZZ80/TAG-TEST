## What it says

A sample of 100 German App Store reviews of DB Navigator from five days, mostly negative. Two things dominate: the app cannot be separated from the railway's delays in users' minds, and at that moment buying the monthly national ticket was blocked by an identity check through a third company. On the journey itself, daily users say delay and platform information is late or wrong, so they behave as if the train were on time; alternatives proposed during a disruption are not workable or make the original trip's status disappear.

## Data and quotes used

- Store rating on 2026-10-07: 4.38 average from 568,853 ratings in the Germany store; app version 26.18.0, released 2026-09-29 (Apple lookup).
- Sample: 100 reviews, 5 stars: 14, 4 stars: 7, 3 stars: 17, 2 stars: 10, 1 stars: 52.
- Sample: 100 reviews, App Store Germany, 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-04; ratings: 14 five-star, 7 four-star, 17 three-star, 10 two-star, 52 one-star (store sample). My count by theme, approximate: about 14 on buying the Deutschlandticket and the identity check; about 12 on the discount card not shown or not applied; about 12 on wrong or late delay, platform and connection information; about 14 on complexity of the interface; about 8 general anger at the railway; about 15 short praise.
- Delay information one cannot act on, DE: 'Selbst wenn der Zug ueber 5 Minuten vor angegebener Verspaetung im Bahnhof steht, wird die Angabe in der App nicht aktualisiert ... Man muss also trotz angezeigter Verspaetung so tun als kaeme der Zug puenktlich, um sicher zu sein' (EN: even when the train stands in the station over 5 minutes before the delay shown, the app is not updated ... so despite the delay shown one must act as if the train came on time, to be safe); 'Ich habe schon so oft den Zug nicht bekommen weil ein falscher Gleiswechsel angezeigt oder eine zu hohe Verspaetung angegeben wurde' (EN: I have missed the train so often because a wrong platform change or too long a delay was shown) (2026-10-01).
- Information arrives too late to use: a commuter from Lueneburg to Hamburg: the delay is announced in the minute the train should leave, the platform change 2-3 minutes before; 'Einfach respektlos' (EN: simply disrespectful) (2026-10-01).
- Alternatives that are not workable: EN-original 'Alternative connections suggested by the app are often not feasible as train delays and cancellations are not taken into account. The information provided is very generic and not helpful when trying to assess alternative options' (2026-09-30).
- Looking at an alternative hides the trip one is on, DE: after tapping the suggested alternative 'hat man keine Ahnung mehr, wie die aktuellen Informationen der urspruenglichen Reise sind ... Echt doof, wenn man ... noch in dem Zug sitzt mit der Verspaetung' (EN: one no longer has any idea of the current status of the original trip ... really bad when one is still sitting in the delayed train) (2026-10-02).
- After a missed connection, DE: 'App aktualisiert sich nicht mit neuen Verbindungen fuer eine gebuchte Strecke, wenn man einen Anschluss verpasst ... Stattdessen schickt sie herzliche Glueckwuensche, wenn der verpasste Zug puenktlich am Ziel ist' (EN: the app does not update with new connections for a booked route when a connection is missed ... it sends congratulations when the missed train reaches the destination on time) (2026-10-02).
- Comparing options is what is missing, DE: 'Mir fehlt eine bequeme Moeglichkeit, Fahrtalternativen ueber mehrere Streckenfuehrungen zu vergleichen. Die staendig doch wieder ueberholten Verspaetungsmeldungen bringen dagegen nichts' (EN: I miss a convenient way to compare alternatives over several routes; the delay messages, constantly out of date again, are of no use) (2026-10-01).
- A daily traveller on changing routes: 'Ich fahre taeglich. Verschiedene Strecken und auch oft die selben Strecken. Staendig fehlen Daten und oder sind falsch. Es werden unmoegliche Verbindungen angezeigt ... Man verpasst trotz Puffer Termine' (EN: I travel daily, different routes and often the same ones. Data are constantly missing or wrong. Impossible connections are shown ... one misses appointments despite a buffer); the filter by means of transport is broken and no service intervals are shown (2026-10-01).
- Two apps for one trip: 'Dadurch das ich nicht mehr sehen kann ob der Bus Verspaetung hat ... weil ich jetzt immer zwischen 2 Apps hin und her switchen muss' (EN: since I can no longer see whether the bus is late ... I now always have to switch between two apps) (2026-10-03). No ticket can be bought for some bus connections to villages (2026-09-30).
- The pass as a barrier: buying the national monthly ticket requires proving identity through a third-party company with access to online banking; many refuse or fail, and pay single tickets meanwhile: 'Es entstanden unnoetige Kosten' (EN: unnecessary costs arose) (2026-09-30, 10-01, several). No ticket in the phone wallet or on paper; a fine when the phone battery is empty (2026-10-02).
- Re-booking after a change of plan by the railway: seat reservations for 4 people had to be redone 8 times (2026-10-04).
- Compensation for delays is the part praised: 'wegen der Verspaetungen haeufig einen Teil des Fahrpreises zurueckbekommen ... Das ist tatsaechlich gut gemacht und leicht zu finden' (2026-10-04); others wait weeks for it (2026-10-03).
- Praise from heavy users exists: the app 'hat mich noch nie im Stich gelassen' (EN: has never let me down), 'Alles aus einer Hand' (EN: everything from one source) (2026-10-01, 09-30). 'It tells me when a train comes late ... but trains coming late is an anxiety test' (2026-10-01).

## Limits

Five days of reviews in a week when a new identity check blocked a purchase: a very skewed moment. Germany, where rail punctuality is poor (see GR-041), so the app is rated for the railway. App store reviews over-represent anger. Several reviewers are daily or frequent travellers, which makes this sample closer to commuters than the other review samples, but none states the distance. Counts by theme are my rough reading of 100 items, not market data. Translations are working translations; some quotes are shortened.
