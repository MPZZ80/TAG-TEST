## What it says

A sample of 100 Dutch App Store reviews of the national railway's app, mostly negative after a redesign. The single biggest event: an update made 'saved trips' disappear from the home screen, and about a quarter of the sample is people asking for them back, calling them the most used function and the only reason to use this app over a general map app. The redesign also hid the platform number and the disruptions. People want the facts of their usual trip at a glance, without extra steps.

## Data and quotes used

- Store rating on 2026-10-07: 4.56 average from 161,385 ratings in the Netherlands store; app version 8.55.0, released 2026-10-05 (Apple lookup).
- Sample: 100 reviews, 5 stars: 13, 4 stars: 8, 3 stars: 15, 2 stars: 22, 1 stars: 42.
- Sample: 100 reviews, App Store Netherlands, 2026-05-30 to 2026-10-03; ratings: 13 five-star, 8 four-star, 15 three-star, 22 two-star, 42 one-star (store sample). My count by theme, approximate: about 24 on saved trips disappearing; about 14 on the redesign being cluttered or slow; about 12 on crashes and loading; about 8 on tickets, passes and discounts; about 6 on wrong or missing disruption information; about 10 praise.
