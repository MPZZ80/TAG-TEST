## What it says

A sample of 100 Italian App Store reviews of the Trenitalia app, two thirds one-star, written after a compulsory redesign. Most are about the app failing at basic tasks: errors, payments taken without a ticket, login lost, a slow QR code. Several reviewers say they are commuters, and they name what they need: changing the ticket to another train, the monthly pass payment working, notifications for their usual train, live train status that is right, compensation requests that work. Buying is blocked five minutes before departure even when the train is late.

## Limits

App store reviews after a compulsory redesign: a skewed moment, with two thirds one-star. Reviews are from iPhone users in Italy. Four or five reviewers say they are commuters, which makes this the sample closest to the project's users, but they do not state distance or whether they change mode. Almost nothing on getting to or from the station. Some complaints are about the railway, not the app. Counts by theme are my rough reading of 100 items, not market data. Translations are working translations.
