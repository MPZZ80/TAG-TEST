## What it says

A sample of 100 Italian App Store reviews of the Italo app, half one-star. Reviewers include weekly travellers and holders of multi-trip carnets on routes such as Turin-Milan. Their complaints: the live train tracking, which they rely on, rarely works; tickets and the QR code take a long time to load; login is lost at every opening; carnets are hard to book. Several say the train service is good and the app is not at its level. One states that for people with connections, delays and platforms kept up to date are essential.

## Limits

App store reviews, skewed by an update in late September 2026 that broke login and merged the business app. A private high-speed operator: travellers on main lines between large cities, more business and frequent travellers than regional commuters. Some reviewers travel weekly or with carnets, which is close to the project's users, but none describes the legs before and after the train. Counts by theme are my rough reading of 100 items, not market data. Translations are working translations.
