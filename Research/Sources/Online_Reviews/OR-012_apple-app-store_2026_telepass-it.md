## What it says

A sample of 50 Italian App Store reviews of Telepass, half one-star and half positive. The positive ones are about paying street parking and car parks without effort. The negative ones are about not being able to log in for weeks, an assistance service nobody can reach, and hidden costs. Nobody in the sample mentions the train, bus or sharing tickets that the app also sells: it is used and judged as a tool for the car.

## Data and quotes used

- Store rating on 2026-10-07: 4.62 average from 77,116 ratings in the Italy store; app version 5.57.1, released 2026-09-30 (Apple lookup).
- Sample: 50 reviews, 5 stars: 20, 4 stars: 1, 3 stars: 2, 2 stars: 3, 1 stars: 24.
- Sample: 50 reviews, App Store Italy, 2026-07-10 to 2026-08-27; ratings: 24 one-star, 3 two-star, 2 three-star, 1 four-star, 20 five-star (store sample). My count by theme, approximate: about 14 on login or access blocked; about 9 on assistance that cannot be reached; about 8 praise for parking payment; about 4 on costs, marketing and contracts; none on public transport, trains or sharing.
- What is valued: 'ritengo comodissima la possibilita di pagare le strisce blu in citta' (EN: I find the possibility to pay blue-line street parking in the city very convenient) (2026-08-20); 'Parcheggio: comodo e senza complicazioni' (2026-08-25); 'Dovrebbe essere esteso a piu strutture' (EN: it should be extended to more car parks) (2026-08-26).
- It fails at the moment of need: 'quando ti serve non c'e mai, si deve aggiornare (centinaia di MB e magari sei sotto la pioggia senza copertura rete cellulare e devi pagare un ... parcheggio strisce blu). Oppure ... ti richiede di accedere e ti richiede tutti i codici, sempre in momenti di urgenza' (EN: when you need it, it is never there, it must update, hundreds of MB, and maybe you are in the rain with no coverage and must pay for street parking; or it asks you to log in with all the codes, always in urgent moments) (2026-07-31); asking for the password 'quando ti serve per pagare ad esempio un parcheggio non e proprio il massimo' (2026-08-04).
- One app per city for parking: 'comune che vai app di parcheggio che trovi: dovresti averne installate 100 se viaggi spesso' (EN: every municipality has its own parking app: you would need 100 installed if you travel often) (2026-07-31); a parking zone missing from the app sends a user back to a competitor (2026-08-11).
- No reminder that the parking session is running: 'non possono mettere un alert ogni ora per far sapere che la sosta e ancora attiva; ogni tanto pago per otto, 10 ore perche mi dimentico' (EN: can they not put an alert every hour to say the parking is still active; sometimes I pay for eight or 10 hours because I forget) (2026-07-13).
- Access and assistance: users locked out for weeks or months after a contract change; the website login requires approval from the app that does not open; 'per ricevere assistenza e un labirinto' (2026-08-06, 08-24, 07-10).
- Trust in the company: 'ti trovi con piani tariffari che non hai mai accettato esplicitamente' (EN: you find yourself on price plans you never explicitly accepted); repeated sales calls (2026-07-26, 07-22).
- A wish to see which vehicle made each trip, 'cosi da poter capire meglio quando si e percorso quel tragitto e con quale mezzo' (EN: to understand when that route was travelled and with which vehicle) (2026-08-11).

## Limits

Only 50 reviews over seven weeks. Car users; the app's train, bus and sharing sections are not mentioned by anyone, so the sample cannot say whether selling all modes in one payment app is used. Access problems dominate and may be temporary. App store reviews over-represent anger. Counts by theme are my rough reading of 50 items. Translations are working translations.
