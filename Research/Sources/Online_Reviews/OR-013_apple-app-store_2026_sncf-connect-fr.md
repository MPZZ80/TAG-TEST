## What it says

A sample of 100 French App Store reviews of the national railway's 'all-in-one' app, half one-star. Users say it shows too much that is commercial and too little of what they need at a glance; discount cards and passes drop out of the profile so people pay full price; delay messages are too many and often wrong; changing a trip with connections is hard. Weekly users value the notifications about delay, cancellation and platform. One review captures trust: a teenager who commutes to school is stressed because he does not trust the app's information.

## Limits

App store reviews over-represent anger. France: one national operator and one sales app, a different market from Italy's two operators. Several reviewers are weekly or regular travellers, but distances are not stated and nobody describes the legs before and after the train. Part of the sample is one bug (ticket on the lock screen). Counts by theme are my rough reading of 100 items, not market data. Translations are working translations; some quotes are shortened.
