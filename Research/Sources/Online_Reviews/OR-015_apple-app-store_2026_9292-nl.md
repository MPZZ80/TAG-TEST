## What it says

A sample of 100 Dutch App Store reviews of the national door-to-door public transport planner, 60% positive. People praise having every operator in one app and say it gets them to school or work on time. The complaints are that times and cancellations do not match what happens at the stop, that the company answers it depends on what operators send, that the search is lost when leaving the app, and advertising. Two stranded travellers show the cost of a silent error in a trip with several changes.

## Data and quotes used

- Store rating on 2026-10-07: 4.48 average from 63,819 ratings in the Netherlands store; app version v2.51.1, released 2026-09-11 (Apple lookup).
- Sample: 100 reviews, 5 stars: 42, 4 stars: 18, 3 stars: 10, 2 stars: 8, 1 stars: 22.
- Sample: 100 reviews, App Store Netherlands, 2025-10-29 to 2026-10-02; ratings: 42 five-star, 18 four-star, 10 three-star, 8 two-star, 22 one-star (store sample). My count by theme, approximate: about 45 short praise; about 16 on times, delays or cancellations not matching reality; about 6 on the app resetting or not loading; about 5 on advertising; about 5 on e-tickets and QR codes; about 4 on missing options or alternatives.
- One app for all operators is the value named: 'alles in 1 app ... bus, train, metro, tram ... ik reis ermee door heel Nederland' (EN: everything in one app ... I travel all over the Netherlands with it) (2026-08-20); 'een prettige app om OV-trips, en met name trips met gecombineerd OV, te plannen en middels e-tickets te regelen ... in een oogopslag de complete reis' (EN: a pleasant app to plan public transport trips, above all combined ones, and arrange them with e-tickets ... the complete trip at a glance) (2026-05-30); 'Betere overstappen en minder reistijd' than the railway's own app (2026-09-20); 'Veel actueler dan NS app. Bij wijzigingen van perron gaat altijd verkeerd met NS app' (EN: much more up to date than the railway's app, which always gets platform changes wrong) (2025-11-27).
- Being on time for school or work is the benefit users state: 'nu weet ik wanneer mijn bus komt voor school'; 'Ik kom nooit meer te laat' (2026-09-20, 01-25, 2025-12-10).
- The planner is blamed for what operators send: 'Schema's worden niet up to date gehouden. Vaak conflicten tussen vervoerders'; the company's reply, quoted by the reviewer: '9292 is een reisplanner en wij zijn daarbij afhankelijk van de actuele informatie die vervoerders met ons delen' (EN: we are a trip planner and depend on the current information that operators share with us); the reviewer calls this pushing the problem onto others (2026-09-17).
- Cancelled or missing buses not shown: 'als een bus niet komt wordt dat nooit aangegeven' (2026-06-18); a route changed and not updated (2026-01-27); an hour's wait in the cold for a bus that never came (2025-12-24); a stop closed and the replacement stop not in the app, bus missed (2026-04-29); 'laat gewoon even overzichtelijk weten waar en wanneer er wel een bus komt' (EN: just tell me clearly where and when a bus does come) when a stop is dropped (2025-11-13).
- A silent error in a chain of three legs: a trip train-bus-bus searched on a Sunday; the last bus does not run at weekends and the app had jumped to Monday without saying so: 'Ik stond dus gestrand op Dokkum busstation. Een taxi was 63 euro' (EN: so I was stranded at the bus station; a taxi was 63 euros) (2026-05-03).
- A strike not represented: trains to the airport were running but the app did not show them: 'I had to take an Uber' (2026-09-09).
- Following the trip under way is missing: 'Ik wil bijv. checken of de overstap nog geldig is op basis van huidige vervoer ... wil dat het gewoon op de scherm blijft met dezelfde overstap informatie en tijden' (EN: I want to check whether the change still holds given the vehicle I am on ... I just want it to stay on the screen with the same change information and times) (2025-11-23); every time one leaves the app for 3 seconds the destinations must be entered again (2026-07-13, 07-10, 07-02).
- Alternatives: 'Bij werkzaamheden of storingen onvoldoende ... alternatief' (EN: with works or disruptions not enough alternatives shown) (2026-02-03); 'alleen maar de snelste manier van reizen weergeeft. Ik zou wat meer reismogelijkheden ook wel fijn vinden' (EN: it only shows the fastest way; I would like more options) (2026-02-13); a user finds better connections by searching each part separately on longer trips (2026-03-14).
- First leg: walking to the station is no longer offered without taking the bus (2026-03-22); walking guidance opens another company's map app, which takes time 'en mis ik soms de aansluiting' (EN: and I sometimes miss the connection) (2025-12-24).
- A trick lost: a user had learned to wait at home by watching the bus's live position; it now stays shown at the starting point (2026-06-01); live positions missing after an update (2025-10-31).
- The e-ticket for the whole trip can fail at the gate: QR codes that do not scan, 'Hierdoor vaak de bus gemist'; after a crash all e-tickets were lost and the user paid twice (2026-04-25, 03-06, 05-30).
- Advertising in a tool people call crucial: 'onmogelijk om je te focussen op bustijden etc door de bewegende en knipperende advertenties ... laden de advertenties beter dan tijden' (EN: impossible to focus on bus times because of moving and flashing adverts ... the adverts load better than the times) (2026-02-10).

## Limits

App store reviews; the sample spans a year, and many positive reviews are a few words, several from school pupils. Netherlands, mostly bus and local trips; distances not stated. The data come from operators, so the planner is judged for their errors, as the company itself replies. Counts by theme are my rough reading of 100 items, not market data. Dutch text read through my working translation; check quotes in the original before reuse.
