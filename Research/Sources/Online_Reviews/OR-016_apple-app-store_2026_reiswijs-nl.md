## What it says

A sample of 20 Dutch App Store reviews of a regional app that rewards sustainable trips. Rewards motivate some users, but cost outweighs them, automatic detection of the means of transport is unreliable, and several users refuse the constant location tracking, one naming the employer as a worry.

## Data and quotes used

- Store rating on 2026-10-07: 4.62 average from 325 ratings in the Netherlands store; app version 1.10.2, released 2026-09-29 (Apple lookup).
- Sample: 20 reviews, 5 stars: 8, 4 stars: 1, 3 stars: 3, 2 stars: 1, 1 stars: 7.
- Sample: 20 reviews, App Store Netherlands, 2025-07-01 to 2026-09-26; ratings: 8 five-star, 1 four-star, 3 three-star, 1 two-star, 7 one-star (store sample). A reward app for sustainable trips backed by regional public programmes.
- Rewards do motivate some: 'Telkens denk ik ik ga met de auto maar pak dan toch de fiets' (EN: each time I think I will take the car, and then I take the bike after all) (2026-08-25); 'het motiveert je om na te denken over je reisbewegingen en modaliteit' (EN: it makes you think about your trips and your mode) (2026-08-10).
- Cost decides in the end: the same motivated user notes that the alternative costs '6,- per dag ... 3-4 x per week', much more than the car, which 'is niet bevorderend voor mij om daar straks mee door te gaan' (EN: does not encourage me to keep going with it) (2026-08-25).
- Constant tracking is the price and several refuse it: 'De app verplicht je om locatievoorziening en bewegingen altijd toestemming te verlenen ... iedere beweging van je geregistreerd'; a wish to start and stop a trip by hand (2026-07-31); 'Kan de werkgever zo inschatten waar er weer bespaard kan worden op reiskosten? Of wat je eigenlijk doet in je vrije tijd? Hij gaat er uit' (EN: can the employer use it to see where travel costs can be cut, or what you do in your free time? It goes off my phone) (2026-07-11); the privacy statement is unclear for an app recommended by government (2026-03-14).
- Automatic detection of the mode is unreliable: a bike ride split into bus rides, walks and bits of cycling (2025-07-07); a train trip to Paris recorded as a car trip (2026-09-26); walks start being recorded 5-10 minutes late (2025-09-25).
- People like to see their own trips: 'Leuk om einde dag mijn reizen terug te zien met de vervoersmethodes' (EN: nice to see my trips with the means of transport at the end of the day) (2025-07-01).

## Limits

Only 20 reviews in 15 months: an anecdotal sample of a small regional app. Urban and regional trips, mostly cycling and walking. Useful for three signals only: rewards nudge some people, cost outweighs rewards, and continuous location tracking is refused by a part of users, with the employer named as a worry. Dutch text read through my working translation.
