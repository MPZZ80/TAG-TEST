## What it says

A sample of 85 Dutch App Store reviews of an independent rail app, almost all positive and mostly written in its launch week by early followers. Users praise its clarity against the railway's own app and at once ask for buses, trams and door-to-door addresses.

## Data and quotes used

- Store rating on 2026-10-07: 4.69 average from 239 ratings in the Netherlands store; app version 2.0.4, released 2026-09-02 (Apple lookup).
- Sample: 85 reviews, 5 stars: 62, 4 stars: 19, 3 stars: 2, 2 stars: 1, 1 stars: 1.
- Sample: 85 reviews, App Store Netherlands, 2026-01-10 to 2026-09-29; ratings: 62 five-star, 19 four-star, 2 three-star, 1 two-star, 1 one-star (store sample). Two thirds of the sample were written in the first ten days after launch (10-21 January 2026), many in one or two words, by people who say they followed the app's development or its beta.
- What is praised: clarity against the railway's own app: 'Veel overzichtelijker en gelijk toegankelijker dan de NS' (EN: much clearer and at once more accessible than the railway's app) (2026-01-13); 'Je ziet in 1 oogopslag wanneer je trein vertrekt en wanneer je aankomt' (EN: you see at a glance when your train leaves and when you arrive) (2026-01-13); the trip stays visible at the bottom of the screen (2026-04-23).
- Points and statistics make some people like the train more: 'ook heb ik meer zin en behoefte om met de trein te gaan' (EN: I also feel more like going by train) (2026-01-10); 'Er worden statistieken bijgehouden waar ik eerder nooit bij stil heb gestaan' (EN: it keeps statistics I had never thought about before) (2026-01-16).
- Train only at launch, and that is the main request: 'Ik mis alleen nog de bussen' (EN: the only thing I miss is the buses), repeated by about eight reviewers; 'Ik reis veel met de bus en tram en nauwelijks met de trein' (2026-06-04); 'jammer dat je alleen naar treinstations kan navigeren en niet naar andere adressen' (EN: a pity that one can only navigate to train stations and not to other addresses) (2026-05-18).
- Delay refunds as a draw: 'Je krijgt 9/10 keer je geld terug van ns door deze app' (EN: nine times out of ten you get your money back from the railway through this app) (2026-05-18); the one critical review says this is only a link to the railway's website and that notifications do not work (2026-01-24).
- A user does not see the difference from the national planner and uses the app only to check train times, buying no tickets in it (2026-02-25).

## Limits

A launch-week sample of an independent app, largely from early followers of the developer: the 95% positive rating is not comparable with the other samples and probably not representative. Short reviews with little content. Netherlands, trains. Useful for two signals: a small independent developer can build a clearer rail app on open data, and users at once ask for buses, trams and door-to-door addresses. Dutch text read through my working translation.
