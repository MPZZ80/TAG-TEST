## What it says

The app of the German railway: information and tickets for local and long-distance transport, underground, tram and bus.

## Data and quotes used

- "Der DB Navigator bietet Ihnen Reiseauskünfte und Tickets für den Nah- und Fernverkehr sowie für U-Bahn, Straßenbahn und Bus." In English: the DB Navigator offers travel information and tickets for local and long-distance transport as well as for underground, tram and bus (working translation).
- "... ob Sie allein reisen, pendeln, mit der Familie oder in der Gruppe unterwegs sind." In English: whether you travel alone, commute, with family or in a group (working translation).
- Own claims (captured 2026-10-07): 'Reiseauskuenfte und Tickets fuer den Nah- und Fernverkehr sowie fuer U-Bahn, Strassenbahn und Bus' (EN: travel information and tickets for regional and long-distance trains and for metro, tram and bus); commuting is named among the uses ('pendeln') (product page).
- Disruption filter: 'Nur fahrbare Verbindungen' (EN: only connections that can actually be travelled) hides connections with cancellations or changes that can no longer be made according to live data and shows 'die naechsten fahrbaren Alternativen' (EN: the next workable alternatives) (product page).
- Alternatives during the trip: a button 'Alternativen suchen' gives other connections to the destination when something deviates; but 'Bitte ueberpruefen Sie vorher, ob Ihr Ticket fuer die ausgewaehlte Alternative gueltig ist' (EN: please check first whether your ticket is valid for the chosen alternative): the app updates the plan and the notifications, not the ticket (product page).
- For commuters: 'Pendlerwidget', notifications on the trip, and saved 'Streckenfavoriten' (EN: favourite routes) with the chosen options (product page).
- Other functions listed: how full the train will be, coach order, self check-in on board, seat reservation, bike ticket, Deutschlandticket with price and connection filter (product page).
- First and last mile: a map of the surroundings with stops and departure boards, the walking route to a stop, and 'Sharing-Dienste': bikes and scooters nearby on the map (found, not booked, as far as the page says) (product page).

## Limits

The operator's own description; it does not say how well the functions work, and user reviews of the same app (see the review sources) should be read next to it. Germany. No car, parking or park and ride. The page admits the ticket is not adapted when the route changes. Not tested; the door-to-door check was not done. Translations are working translations.
