## What it says

A Korean mobility super-app by Kakao Mobility covering taxi, chauffeur, bike, parking, navigation, intercity bus, train, air and express delivery.

## Data and quotes used

- "택시. 대리. 바이크. 주차. 내비. 시외버스. 기차. 항공. 퀵/택배" In English: taxi, chauffeur, bike, parking, navigation, intercity bus, train, air, express/parcel (working translation).
- "모든 이동을 위한 모빌리티 서비스를 제공합니다." In English: we provide mobility services for all movement (working translation).
- Own claim (captured 2026-10-07), KO: '모든 이동을 위한 모빌리티 서비스를 제공합니다. 택시. 대리. 바이크. 주차. 내비. 시외버스. 기차. 항공. 퀵/택배' (EN: we provide mobility services for every trip: taxi, designated driver, bike, parking, navigation, intercity bus, train, flight, courier) (product page).
- The list puts car services (taxi, driver, parking, navigation) and collective transport between cities (intercity bus, train, flight) in one app; urban bus and metro are not in the list on this page (product page).
- News headlines on the page are about self-driving service trials, defence technology and holiday travel tips (product page).

## Limits

Corporate home page with slogans only: it names the services, not how they are joined (one plan, one ticket, one payment, or separate sections). South Korea; one dominant company tied to the main messaging app, a context with no equivalent in Italy. Korean text read through my working translation. Not tested; the door-to-door check was not done.
