## What it says

A Dutch multimodal planner that bundles the timetables of train, bus, tram, metro and ferry in one app, with e-tickets, live locations and delay information (listing text).

## Data and quotes used

- "9292 bundelt alle dienstregelingen voor trein, bus, tram, metro en veerboot in Nederland in één app. Plan je reis, koop je e-ticket, volg live locaties en blijf op de hoogte van vertragingen." In English: 9292 bundles all timetables for train, bus, tram, metro and ferry in the Netherlands in one app; plan your trip, buy your e-ticket, follow live locations and stay informed about delays (App Store text; working translation).
- Own claims in the store listing (captured 2026-10-07), NL: '9292 bundelt alle dienstregelingen voor trein, bus, tram, metro en veerboot in Nederland in een app' (EN: brings together all timetables for train, bus, tram, metro and ferry in the Netherlands in one app); live information from more than 15 named operators; 'Meer dan 5 miljoen gebruikers'; 'Al meer dan 30 jaar' (store listing).
- Disruption: 'Bij werkzaamheden of uitval geeft de app automatisch een alternatief reisadvies' (EN: with works or cancellations the app automatically gives an alternative travel advice) (store listing).
- One ticket: 'E-ticket voor je hele reis' (EN: e-ticket for your whole trip), with the cost shown at once and a QR code to open the gates (store listing).
- First and last mile inside the plan: 'Begin of eindig je reis op de fiets of scooter' (EN: start or end your trip by bike or scooter): the user says whether to walk, cycle or use a scooter at the start or the end, also with a shared or electric bike, and 'De planner toont automatisch beschikbare verhuurlocaties in de buurt' (EN: the planner shows available rental points nearby); one brand of shared bike can be rented in the app (store listing).
- Also: live vehicle positions, expected crowding, favourite routes on the home screen for 'een reisadvies in een klik' (EN: advice in one click) (store listing).

## Limits

Store listing by the company, which is owned by the Dutch public transport operators. Netherlands: one national timetable and real-time feed and one payment system, which make this possible. Public transport, bikes and scooters; no car or parking. Dutch text read through my working translation. Not tested; the door-to-door check was not done.
