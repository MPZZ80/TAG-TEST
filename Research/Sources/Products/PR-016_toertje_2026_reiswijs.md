## What it says

A commuting app that tracks trips automatically and turns sustainable choices (walking, cycling, public transport, carpooling, working from home) into points (listing text).

## Data and quotes used

- "ReisWijs maakt van slimmer, groener en gezonder reizen door Amsterdam en Zuid-Holland iets dat loont. Wandel, pak de fiets, spring op het OV, rij samen of werk gewoon thuis." In English: ReisWijs makes smarter, greener and healthier travel through Amsterdam and South Holland rewarding; walk, take the bike, hop on public transport, ride together or simply work from home (App Store text; working translation).
- Own claims in the store listing (captured 2026-10-07): an app for Amsterdam and Zuid-Holland that rewards 'slimmer, groener en gezonder reizen' (EN: smarter, greener, healthier travel): walking, cycling, public transport, riding together 'of werk gewoon thuis' (EN: or simply work from home) (store listing).
- It records trips automatically ('RitRadar') and turns each sustainable choice into points for vouchers and free transport tickets; challenges for companies and users (store listing).
- It warns of road works on the user's route and points to an alternative (store listing).
- Made 'In samenwerking met Amsterdam Bereikbaar & Zuid-Holland Bereikbaar' (EN: with two regional accessibility programmes): a public programme to reduce congestion, not a commercial planner (store listing).

## Limits

Short store listing. A reward scheme tied to regional road-works programmes, launched in 2025; no evidence here on use or effect. Netherlands. It counts working from home as a rewarded choice, which says the aim is fewer car trips at peak time, not better trips. Dutch text read through my working translation. Not tested.
