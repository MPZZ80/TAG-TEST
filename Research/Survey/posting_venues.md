# Where to Post the Survey

Places where the commuter survey could be posted, by language, with who to ask first. Links opened on 2026-10-12 unless marked.

- **Permission:** "ask first" means the place is run by an association or by moderators, and a post from a stranger without their consent is likely to be removed. Write to the contact given, say who you are, what the survey is for, that it is anonymous and takes 5 minutes, and attach the link.
- **What was checked:** that the page exists and what contact it shows. The rules of each group on surveys were not checked, and Reddit and Facebook cannot be read by the agent: read the rules of each group before posting.
- **E-mail addresses** are written as the page shows them. Where the page hides the address or has only a form, this is said.

## Left out

- **Spanish:** the Catalan association for public transport, [transportpublic.org](http://www.transportpublic.org/), is a member of the European federation. No Spanish version of the survey was made; say if it is wanted.
- **Closed Facebook and Telegram groups of single lines:** they exist for most Italian lines and cannot be read by the agent. The committees above can point to them.
