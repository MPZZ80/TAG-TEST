# Open Issues

What the research has not settled: open questions, weak sources and contradictions. It is built from the source notes and the interview notes as they are on 2026-10-12. The ratings are the agent's proposal for Marco to correct.

## How to read it

- **Typology:** the three sections below. Inside each, the items are ordered by priority; the identifiers do not change when an item moves.
- **Priority** is about when: `high` before the next interviews or before any synthesis is written; `medium` before the findings are used for a decision; `low` when there is time.
- **Criticality** is about the damage if it stays as it is: `critical` it could change what the project concludes about its user or its problem; `major` it weakens one line of evidence; `minor` it affects one figure or one note.
- An item can be urgent and small, or large and not urgent: the two columns are independent.
