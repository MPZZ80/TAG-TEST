# Brief

## User

The user is a regular or semi-regular commuter who travels between cities and combines different mobility options, including public transport, private vehicles and other mobility services.

## Problem

The core problem is that commuters often experience friction both when planning a journey and when dealing with unexpected situations during the trip, while information and available mobility options are fragmented across different services.

## Motivation

This problem interests me because I experience intercity commuting myself and I am interested in understanding how digital services could reduce uncertainty, support better decisions, and improve both journey planning and problem solving across different mobility options.
